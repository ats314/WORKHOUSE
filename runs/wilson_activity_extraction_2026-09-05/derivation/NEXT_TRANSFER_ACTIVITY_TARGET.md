# Exact transported-transfer activities and the next quantitative target

## 1. Inputs and precise remaining norm

The inputs are the rooted-contraction theorem, the actual symmetric
creators and their uniformly gapped parent in
`paper/research_notes/G18_WILSON_CREATOR_PARENT_AND_SPECTRAL_FLOW_20260905.md`,
and the operator bridge in
`paper/research_notes/G18_EXCITED_WINDOW_OPERATOR_BRIDGE_20260904.md`, Section 5.
The parent theorem supplies an exact finite-volume vacuum unitary through
a common spectral filter, and a quasi-local thermodynamic automorphism.
It supplies no actual Wilson excited-spectrum estimate.

The bridge's basic hypothesis, equation (13), is

    eta = sup_i sum_{X contains i} delta^(-|X|) ||F_X||,
    delta = 4/5,  eta <= 1/400 being a sufficient threshold.

This is exponential SUPPORT CARDINALITY weighting. It is not a diameter
norm. Additional spatial and source weights appear after the basic
operator theorem. Consequently the available almost-exponential diameter
decay for the NSY spectral flow does not, by itself, imply even the basic
activity norm, before the sharper spatial/source requirements are added.
The canonical proof correctly leaves the operator estimate open; this
distinction sharpens the explanation of why it remains open.

Here is a direct separation of the two norms. Let B_R be concentric balls
in the three-dimensional link graph, and assign one self-adjoint activity
F_{B_R}=epsilon_R Q_{B_R}, where Q_{B_R}=I-P_{B_R} and
epsilon_R=epsilon exp(-sqrt(R)). These activities annihilate their local
vacua and have summable stretched-exponential diameter tails, even after
any fixed polynomial loss. But |B_R| is bounded below by c R^3 for large R,
so sum_R (5/4)^|B_R| epsilon_R diverges for every epsilon>0. The same
comparison holds for an almost-exponential tail such as
exp(-R/log(R+2)^2). This is a counterexample to the implication between
these bounds for a given activity decomposition, not a counterexample for
the actual Wilson transfer or a proof that an alternative decomposition
cannot have a better norm.

## 2. A finite-volume extraction theorem

Let the sites be links in a fixed finite ambient set Lambda, with arbitrary
Hilbert spaces H_i, product vacua Omega_X, and bounded self-adjoint free
kernels D_i fixing Omega_i. For every subset X of Lambda, suppose a bounded
self-adjoint operator G_X on H_X is specified, with

    G_empty = 1,  G_{i}=D_i,  G_X Omega_X=Omega_X.

This is a family of induced-subsystem operators. No equality between
partial traces of G_X and smaller G_Y is assumed. Embed operators in any
larger tensor product by the identity on the missing factors.

For a nonempty X define its partition cumulant

    C_X = sum_{pi partition of X}
          (-1)^(|pi|-1) (|pi|-1)! tensor_{B in pi} G_B.

Set

    F_{i}=0,  F_X=C_X for |X|>=2.

Then all F_X are bounded and self-adjoint, they satisfy

    F_X P_X = P_X F_X = 0,

and, simultaneously for every Gamma subset of Lambda,

    G_Gamma = sum_{A a family of pairwise disjoint nonempty subsets of Gamma}
              (tensor_{X in A} F_X) tensor
              (tensor_{i in Gamma outside union A} D_i).             (A)

Singleton activities vanish, so they can be omitted from the sum. The
empty activity family contributes the entire free product kernel. This is
exactly the bridge's expansion (12). The family F is unique with F_i=0
and property (A) for all subsystems.

### Proof, including the noncommutative point

Introduce central commuting variables z_i with z_i^2=0, and write
z_X=product_{i in X} z_i. Form the finite formal polynomial

    Z = sum_{X subset of Lambda} G_X z_X.

It has constant term I, so log Z and exp(log Z) are finite polynomials in
the nilpotent ideal generated by the variables. Products with overlapping
supports are zero because they contain some z_i^2. Products which survive
have pairwise disjoint supports, so their tensor operator coefficients
commute. Thus the coefficient of z_X in log Z is exactly C_X: each
partition into k blocks occurs k! times in (Z-I)^k and the logarithm gives
the factor (-1)^(k-1)/k. No product of overlapping G_B is commuted.

The identity Z=exp(sum_X C_X z_X) gives the partition inversion

    G_Gamma = sum_{pi partition of Gamma} tensor_{X in pi} C_X.

Since C_i=G_i=D_i, separating singleton blocks gives (A). Alternatively,
the term F_Gamma is the only term in (A) not involving a proper subset of
Gamma, which proves uniqueness by induction.

Every product in C_X is a tensor product of self-adjoint operators, so
C_X is self-adjoint. Each such product fixes Omega_X. On the invariant
vacuum line the polynomial Z is therefore product_i(1+z_i), whose
logarithm is sum_i z_i. Hence C_X Omega_X=0 for |X|>=2. Self-adjointness
gives annihilation from the left. F_i=0 handles the singletons.

### Connected cancellation under a real component factorization

Suppose X=A disjoint-union B, both parts nonempty, and every induced
subsystem obeys

    G_S = G_{S intersect A} tensor G_{S intersect B},   S subset of X. (B)

Then Z_X=Z_A Z_B. The two polynomials commute because their operator
coefficients and variables act on disjoint sites. Therefore
log Z_X=log Z_A+log Z_B, and C_X=F_X=0. This cancellation is exact at the
chosen real coupling. It does not use Taylor expansion or analyticity of
the spectral-flow unitary.

## 3. Why the actual Wilson family has the required properties

For every finite link set X retain exactly the plaquettes whose entire
four-link support is contained in X. Use the same calibrated one-link
kinetic kernels, same coupling path, same temporal mesh and integer block
m, and the same spectral filter on all these induced models. Put

    T_X = exp(tau u V_X/2) exp(-tau K_X) exp(tau u V_X/2),
    B_X = T_X^m,  b_X = its Perron eigenvalue,
    G_X = U_X^* B_X U_X / b_X.

Here U_X(0)=I is the actual creator-parent spectral flow. Its gap cutoff is
chosen once below the common parent gap 247/256. Work on the real coupling
domain |u|<=u_star/8 used in the canonical parent theorem.

Arbitrary induced finite systems satisfy the contraction hypotheses: four
distinct links per retained plaquette and at most four plaquettes at any
link. Deleting plaquettes preserves these bounds. The real rotor transfer
still has the stated compactness, self-adjointness and positivity-improving
kernel. The parent construction therefore transports its actual Perron
line. Hence G_X is bounded, positive and self-adjoint and fixes Omega_X.
A single link has no magnetic plaquette, so its creators vanish, its
parent path is constant, U_i=I, b_i=1, and

    G_i = exp(-m tau K_i) = D_i.

Now suppose the induced plaquette hypergraph on X is disconnected across
A and B. Then all induced transfers tensorize across that split and
b_S=b_{S intersect A} b_{S intersect B}. The normalized vacuum tensors
also factor. Uniqueness of the exact-support creator logarithm gives
the creator family as the sum of the component families, so the parent
is H_A tensor I + I tensor H_B.

The common-filter spectral-flow generator is a linear filtered integral
of the time-evolved derivative of this parent. Evolution of a term in A
under H_A+H_B is just its evolution under H_A, tensored with I_B, and vice
versa. Thus the generator is the sum of the component generators. The
ODE with U(0)=I gives U_X=U_A tensor U_B exactly; there is no undetermined
phase in this statement. The same argument applies to every S subset X.
Consequently (B) holds, and F_X vanishes on disconnected induced
plaquette supports.

For open or periodic finite geometries all of this is finite tensor
algebra, with their actual retained plaquettes. No ambient metric is
used. A nonzero activity support can be taken to be a connected union
of retained plaquettes: any link in no retained plaquette is an isolated
component and forces the cumulant to vanish.

For such a support the induced link-adjacency graph is connected, so
diam(X)<=|X|-1. Thus, if the target eta is proved, it also bounds the
unaugmented spatial activity norm
sum_{X contains i} exp(beta diam(X)) ||F_X|| for beta<=log(5/4).
A spatial refinement which retains BOTH (5/4)^|X| and an extra factor
exp(beta diam(X)) needs a corresponding extra cardinality margin.
Transformed-source estimates do not follow from this observation.

This proves existence, local vacuum annihilation and connected
subtraction of actual transported-transfer activities. It does not bound
their norms or assert that G_Y is the operator restriction of G_X.
In particular U_Y need not be the restriction of U_X. The induced family,
rather than that false restriction property, is what defines the
cumulants and makes (A) exact.

## 4. The cheapest next calculation and the result it must settle

The substantive target is now purely quantitative: prove a common bound

    sup_{Lambda,epsilon,i} sum_{X subset Lambda, X contains i}
         (5/4)^|X| ||F_X(u)|| <= C |u|

on an explicit nonzero interval, with C |u|<=1/400. This would activate
the full-operator part of the bridge for the actual dressed Wilson block.
Source transport and the sharper spatial matching remain separate bounds.

The next bounded calculation is the full operator coefficient on one
plaquette and on a pair of intersecting plaquettes, using independent
couplings on the active plaquettes before setting them equal. At first
order the new parent flow agrees with the local vacuum rotation already
proved in the operator bridge, Section 4: the free parent is N=sum q_i,
and for a four-link creator a'_p one has [N,a'_p]=4a'_p. The gap filter's
inverse-frequency identity at this nonzero frequency gives the initial
anti-Hermitian generator a'_p-a'_p^*. Thus this order is an established
normalization check, not a new target.

At second order, write the normalized undressed block as
D(u)=D_0+u D_1+u^2 D_2+o(u^2), and U'(u)=S(u)U(u), with
S(u)=S_0+u S_1+o(u). Direct multiplication gives the exact coefficient

    [u^2](U^* D U) = D_2 + [D_1,S_0]
                       + (1/2)[[D_0,S_0],S_0]
                       + (1/2)[D_0,S_1].                         (C)

Differentiate the finite-subsystem parent filtered integral to compute
S_1. The filter has integrable time moments and the finite parent and
its coupling derivatives are bounded, so this finite differentiation is
legitimate. Formula (C) keeps the necessary second-order chart correction;
using exp(u S_0) alone would omit its last term.

For disjoint active plaquettes, component factorization forces their
connected cumulant to vanish exactly. For an intersecting pair p,q, take
X=p union q and extract the mixed u_p u_q coefficient after the explicit
partition subtraction. No proper induced subsystem contains both whole
plaquettes, so the mixed coefficient of F_X is the mixed coefficient of
G_X itself. Same-plaquette repetition belongs to F_p. These are the full
second-order connected geometries. The calculation should keep bounded
magnetic operators and exact free resolvent/semigroup expressions, not
replace a full operator norm by selected matrix elements.

A successful second-order computation would establish its own uniform
coefficient estimate and identify which generator/normalizer terms
produce the cancellations. It would not prove the all-orders bound. The
settling all-orders argument must control the real-coupling remainder or
an absolutely convergent linked operator expansion in this cardinality
norm. Holomorphy of the creator coordinates does not automatically give
holomorphy of the chosen spectral-flow unitary in that norm. No such step
is assumed in the finite extraction theorem above.

## 5. Evidence boundary and reproducibility

Sections 2 and 3 are analytic tensor-algebra arguments under the explicit
finite-family and existing parent hypotheses. They require neither a
representation cutoff nor a global condition number for exp(sum a_X).
The companion exact rational finite control
`check_transfer_activity_mobius.py` tests partition inversion, local
annihilation and disconnected cancellation, and includes overlapping
noncommuting activities. It is a finite algebra control, not a proof of
the actual Wilson activity norm or of spectral-flow locality.

The exact replay passed on a three-site overlapping model and a four-site
disconnected product model: 24 subsystem expansion checks, 22 local
anchoring checks, nine disconnected-zero checks, and a verified nonzero
commutator between overlapping activities. The two-site toy interactions
test the abstract tensor lemma, not the four-link Wilson geometry.
