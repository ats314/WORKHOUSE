## Bounded mathematical reads that affect the next target

1. **A valid local weighting mechanism:** `09_ARCHIVE/sorted_second_pass/07_smooth_proxy_no_volume_leak.md`, hash `9e3371e7e4d4...`, lines 12–65. For a smooth plaquette proxy `z>=0`, bounded `|Delta z_p|<=C`, and `Phi(s)=s^2`, the Laplacian contribution is `2 sum z_p Delta z_p <= 2C sum z_p`. This removes a naked volume term. It is useful for organizing retained-region cutoffs in the current interacting scale problem. It does not prove the negative drift/coercivity term, a quantum-ground estimate, or a small bad-region gradient-energy tail. Those are precisely the additional inputs current G19 needs. The document itself preserves that boundary.

2. **An exact flow with a different operation:** `09_ARCHIVE/sorted_second_pass/EXTRACT_03_GradientFlow_as_RG.md`, hash `da4b0a4e65d2...`, sections 1–2 and 6–7. For the smooth deterministic vector field `b=-g0^2 grad S_W`, density transport gives `partial_t S_t = div b - b dot grad S_t = g0^2(grad S_W dot grad S_t - Delta S_W)`, as printed. Finite-time flow on a compact smooth configuration manifold is invertible. Thus this identity can help track action/score evolution but does not itself eliminate spatial modes, identify the quantum-ground marginal, or supply the current endpoint-history hierarchy. The eight-loop truncation explicitly lacks a remainder bound; its numerical coupling formulas are not evidence for that missing estimate.

3. **A specific uniqueness failure:** `09_ARCHIVE/sorted_second_pass/Theorem_Uniqueness_Scaling_Limit_Wilson_Loops.md`, hash `c5186bd33384...`, lines 62–89. Step 2 bounds the difference between expectations under two distinct measures by invoking tightness and a subsequential weak limit. Tightness does not make that term uniformly small for all scales. On one compact group take densities `1+epsilon chi` and `1-epsilon chi` with respect to Haar, with bounded real mean-zero `chi` and sufficiently small epsilon, alternate them along the sequence, and take the same observable `F=chi` at every scale. The sequence is tight, uniformly integrable, and has zero observable-approximation error, but its expectations alternate by `2 epsilon integral chi^2`. The repair requires a consistency/coupling/Cauchy estimate for the measures, or a separate uniqueness theorem. The claim that rough `H^{-s}` fields admit a separating Wilson-loop algebra also needs a defined measurable holonomy construction. This file does not close the continuum part of G19; preserve it as a precise failed route rather than deleting it.
