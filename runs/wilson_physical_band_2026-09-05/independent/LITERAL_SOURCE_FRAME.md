## 2. Tagged anchored source norm



of the total dressed source alone would not justify the disjoint-support

## 4. Complete projected range, including infinite dimension

The following elementary operator lemma is the totality step.

Let `J0:E ->H` be an isometry with range `ran P0`, and let `Pi` be an
orthogonal projection on `H`. Suppose

```text
d=||Pi-P0||<1,
j=||J-J0||,
d+j<1.
```

Define the bounded coefficient-space operator

```text
B=J0* Pi J.
```

Then

```text
||B-I|| <=d+j<1,
```

so `B` has a Neumann inverse. For any `y in ran Pi`, put
`a=B^(-1)J0*y`. The vector `x=Pi J a-y` belongs to `ran Pi` and
satisfies `J0*x=0`, hence `P0x=0`. Therefore

```text
||x||=||(Pi-P0)x||<=d||x||,
```

which forces `x=0`. This proves `Pi J` is **onto the entire range of
Pi**. It did not infer onto from a lower bound, use a finite-rank
dimension count, or define the physical shell to be the source span.

For every `a`,

```text
(1-d-j)||a|| <=||Pi J a|| <=(1+j)||a||.                 (13)
```

With `d<1/4` and `j<1/8`, (13) gives (4), injectivity and a bounded
inverse. Explicitly, the inverse on `ran Pi` is

```text
(Pi J)^(-1)=B^(-1)J0* restricted to ran Pi,
||(Pi J)^(-1)||<=8/5.
```

Thus the projected literal plaquette sources form a complete Riesz basis
of the full shell, in finite or infinite dimension. The canonical
orthonormal frame is `Pi J (J*Pi J)^(-1/2)` if desired; its existence
does not change which original literal sources generate the range.

## 5. Physical interpretation and remaining matching obligations

In finite volume, the state-preserving chart represents the actual
Wilson vacuum by the product vector and a literal observable `O` by
`V* O V`. Thus the columns of `J` are precisely the literal Wilson
plaquette source vectors in that representation, with no newly chosen
nonlocal substitute sources. Physical gauge, one-form and charge
symmetries are preserved by the chart.

For infinite volume, use the independent strong-limit construction of
the actual normalized blocked transfer on the incomplete product tensor
space and its identification with the selected Wilson GNS transfer by
joint local-observable and transfer-moment limits. The chart's local
source convergence then identifies the same columns of `J` with the
literal-source GNS vectors. Formula (5), followed by Section 4, proves
that their projected span is the full physical shell.

The conclusion is a fixed calibrated temporal mesh and spatial lattice
statement on one rank-dependent coupling interval, uniform over admitted
meshes and volumes. It does not yet prove sharp temporal matching of
projected energy kernels, relative carrier-sheet isolation at Gamma,
or a spatial continuum limit. Those require their separately stated
energy-domain, momentum and scaling hypotheses.

The norm estimates, Schur argument and onto lemma are analytic proofs.
An exact rational control can verify the numerical margins and a finite
matrix model can check the onto construction, but neither is a substitute
for the infinite tagged-family bounds and the operator-limit input.
