# The true-vacuum literal fast floor with one common Gauss constraint

5 September 2026. Analytic additive common-Gauss theorem. The Hamiltonian is a
sum of actual adjacent-two-square Wilson block Hamiltonians. The blocks
have disjoint edges and one common residual SU(N) conjugation constraint.
There are no plaquette interactions between blocks. All uniformity below
concerns the number of these additive copies, for a fixed N.

The exact local source construction and its one-block physical cutoff
argument are in
[the literal-source theorem](G19_WILSON_LITERAL_VACUUM_COARSE_SOURCES_20260905.md),
Sections 1--4. The actual local operator, physical spectrum and harmonic
localization are in the canonical
[G19_WILSON_TWO_SQUARE_PHYSICAL_SHELLS_20260905.md](G19_WILSON_TWO_SQUARE_PHYSICAL_SHELLS_20260905.md).
The present note extends the local argument to its full slow adjoint
multiplet, proves a general tensor projection lemma, and accounts for the
complete common-Gauss low spectrum. It does not identify this equal-time
source space with the reducing space generated by all OS histories.

## 1. A tensor projection estimate without a rank or volume factor

Let H_i have a distinguished unit vector Omega_i and an orthogonal
projection P_i satisfying P_i Omega_i=Omega_i. Put R_i=I-|Omega_i><Omega_i|.
Then P_i commutes with R_i. For a finite set I of factors define

```text
H_S = tensor_(i in S) R_i H_i tensor_(i not in S) C Omega_i,
P_I = tensor_i P_i,       Q_I=I-P_I.                         (1)
```

The exact excitation-support spaces H_S are mutually orthogonal and
reduce P_I and Q_I. This assertion uses exact vacuum inclusion, not a
small vacuum-overlap error.

For each i choose E_i<=R_i, with

```text
E_i P_i E_i >= (1-epsilon_i^2) E_i,    0<=epsilon_i<=1.
```

Let E_S be the corresponding tensor subspace inside H_S. Compressing the
tensor product to E_S gives, exactly,

```text
E_S P_I E_S = tensor_(i in S) (E_i P_i E_i)
            >= product_(i in S)(1-epsilon_i^2) E_S.          (2)
```

Consequently, on any closed subspace L of the orthogonal sum of E_S with
|S|<=k,

```text
Pi_L P_I Pi_L >= (1-epsilon^2)^k Pi_L,
||Q_I Pi_L||^2 <= 1-(1-epsilon^2)^k <= k epsilon^2,           (3)
epsilon=sup_i epsilon_i (a maximum for finitely many factors).
```

There is no restriction on superpositions inside L. In particular its
rank and the number of admissible support sets do not enter (3). If a
compact group acts on every factor, fixes each Omega_i and commutes with
P_i and E_i, the same inequalities hold on the jointly invariant part of
L. A common Gauss constraint does not alter this estimate.

Proof: (2) is a tensor product of positive compressed operators. The
support spaces reduce P_I, so summing (2) gives a block diagonal form
inequality; compress it to L. Finally Q_I is a projection, so
||Q_I Pi_L||^2=||Pi_L Q_I Pi_L||. This proof is stronger than estimating a
sum of separate source-vector errors.

For countably many factors use the vacuum incomplete tensor product.
The finite products of P_i converge strongly to P_infinity. On a vector
with finitely many nonvacuum factors this product is eventually constant.
Equations (1)--(3) pass to the orthogonal exact-support decomposition,
including arbitrary square-summable superpositions of support sets.

## 2. An exact spectral and representation lemma

Assume identical factors H, carrying a compact group G, with an invariant
nonnegative self-adjoint h and unique invariant vacuum Omega. Suppose:

1. The first nonvacuum eigenspace A is an irreducible self-dual
   representation with a one-dimensional invariant space in A tensor A.
   Its energy is alpha>0. The restriction to
   (C Omega plus A)^perp has spectrum in [beta,infinity), beta>alpha.
2. The local invariant space H^G has a simple first excited vector r of
   energy a, and all its other nonvacuum spectrum is at least b>a.
3. P is an equivariant orthogonal projection fixing Omega exactly.

Set

```text
t=min{b,alpha+beta,3alpha},       max{a,2alpha}<t.            (4)
```

On M additive copies let h_M=sum_i h_i, with the single simultaneous G
action. Its complete invariant spectral subspace below t consists of

```text
Omega_M;
r_i = r on factor i, exact vacuum on all other factors;
s_ij = the unit invariant vector in A_i tensor A_j,
       exact vacuum on all other factors,       i<j.        (5)
```

Its dimension is 1+M+M(M-1)/2. Their energies are respectively 0,a,2alpha.
This remains a complete description for countably many copies, with the
two families interpreted as orthogonal l2 direct sums.

Here is the full exclusion argument. Exact excitation support commutes
with both h_M and G. Three or more nonvacuum factors cost at least
3alpha>=t. With two factors, any local component outside A costs at least
alpha+beta>=t. The only remaining two-factor invariant is the specified
singlet. With one factor, invariance is exactly the local Gauss condition,
so only r lies between the vacuum and t<=b. In particular a fast adjoint
on one block is not a physical state by itself. A higher slow quadratic
irrep on one block is likewise not invariant; pairing it with another
excitation does not evade the alpha+beta bound. No local nontrivial
representation is silently thrown away.

Write

```text
d_r^2 = ||(I-P)r||^2,
E_A P E_A = (1-d_A^2) E_A.                                (6)
```

The second identity follows from Schur's lemma; its coefficient lies in
[0,1], and d_A=||(I-P)E_A||. Since P fixes Omega, Q_M preserves every
support in (5). For a pair, compression of P_i tensor P_j to A_i tensor
A_j is the scalar (1-d_A^2)^2. Therefore, with

```text
d_pair^2=1-(1-d_A^2)^2=2d_A^2-d_A^4,                       (7)
```

the exact literal Gram operator on (5) is

```text
Pi_M P_M Pi_M
 = 1 on the vacuum;
   1-d_r^2 on each r_i;
   (1-d_A^2)^2 on each s_ij.                              (8)
```

All off-diagonal entries between these displayed sectors vanish. Thus
for M>=2, including M=infinity,

```text
||Q_M Pi_M||^2 = max{d_r^2,d_pair^2}.                       (9)
```

For M=1 the exact value is d_r^2; the maximum in (9) still provides a
valid bound. Equation (8) controls arbitrary superpositions of the
M(M-1)/2 cross-block singlets, rather than each singlet separately.

The restricted form of h_M on ran Q_M, after the true product-vacuum
energy has been subtracted, has the lower bound

```text
F_M >= f I,
f=t-max{(t-a)d_r^2, (t-2alpha)d_pair^2}.                   (10)
```

For M=1 omit the second term for the sharper one-block bound. To prove
(10), the complete spectral classification gives

```text
h_M >= t(I-|Omega_M><Omega_M|)
       -(t-a)R_rad-(t-2alpha)R_pair.
```

Q_M annihilates Omega_M. The images Q_M r_i and Q_M s_ij lie in mutually
orthogonal exact-support spaces; within each such space the displayed
low invariant vector is unique. Thus the compression of the two deficit
terms has operator norm equal to the maximum in (10), not their sum.

Let J_M be any isometry onto ran P_M. If d_r,d_A<1, then

```text
Pi_M J_M J_M* Pi_M >= c Pi_M,
c=min{1-d_r^2,(1-d_A^2)^2}>0.                             (11)
```

Hence Pi_M J_M is onto the whole low spectral space, with right inverse
(Pi_M J_M)*[(Pi_M J_M)(Pi_M J_M)*]^-1 of norm at most c^-1/2.
This is valid at infinite rank. It uses the lower bound on B B*, not a
lower bound on B* B.

## 3. The local unrestricted Wilson input

Take G=SU(N), N>=2, d=N^2-1. Use the actual seven-link Hamiltonian on
the full Haar space L2(G^2), before imposing its residual Gauss constraint:

```text
H_u = -(1/2)[3Delta_1+3Delta_2+sum_a(L_1,a-R_2,a)^2]
      +2u[v(U1)+v(U2)],         v(U)=N-ReTr U,
h_u=H_u-E0(u).                                             (12)
```

Strict ellipticity on the compact connected manifold and positivity of
the heat kernel give a unique normalized positive smooth vacuum Omega_u.
It is invariant by uniqueness, so its energy is also the local physical
vacuum energy. The operator is equivariant under simultaneous conjugation.

The canonical physical shell proof supplies a=E1^phys-E0 and
b=E2^phys-E0, with

```text
a/sqrt(u) -> 2sqrt(3),       b/sqrt(u) -> sqrt(3)+sqrt(5),  (13)
```

and a simple physical vector r_u converging to the normalized radial
slow oscillator polynomial times the oscillator vacuum.

For clarity, the following unrestricted extension uses the actual local
elliptic form, not the physical-only inversion identity. In exponential
coordinates the electric cometric is smooth and

```text
C(x)=C(0)+O_N(|x|),
C(0)=[[4,-1],[-1,4]] tensor I_d.
```

The potential has a unique global zero and a positive quadratic Hessian.
Write h=u^-1/2 and P_h=h^2 H_E+2(v(U1)+v(U2)). An invariant IMS partition
with radius h^(1/3) has kinetic localization error O(h^(4/3)). On the
inner chart the smooth metric comparison costs O(h^(1/3)) relative to
the kinetic form, the quartic potential error costs O(h^(2/3)) relative
to its quadratic part, and the smooth Haar-density comparison has the
same or smaller order. The potential outside the chart is at least a
constant times h^(2/3), larger than every fixed O(h) low window.
Gaussian Hermite trials and the min-max lower bound consequently give,
for each fixed ordered unrestricted eigenvalue,

```text
E_j(H_u)/sqrt(u) -> e_j(H_osc),
H_osc=-(3/2)Delta_q-(5/2)Delta_z+(|q|^2+|z|^2)/2.          (14)
```

For example these comparisons give the sufficient weaker error
O_N(u^(1/3)) for each fixed eigenvalue. The stronger physical
O_N(u^(1/4)) remainder is neither needed nor imported into the
unrestricted statement.

The same compactness proof gives convergence of each isolated finite
oscillator cluster after local Haar flattening and rescaling. One can
see the representation content without choosing arbitrary eigenvectors:
embed the target Hermite space using invariant smooth chart cutoffs,
project it onto the corresponding actual spectral cluster, and use the
polar part of the resulting map. Convergence makes this map invertible
for large u; every step commutes with G. Thus the actual and harmonic
cluster representations are equivalent.

The first excited oscillator cluster consists of the d linear q
polynomials at gap sqrt(3), carries one copy of the adjoint, and is
separated from the next cluster at sqrt(5). Therefore for all large u
there is an actual d-dimensional irreducible slow adjoint A_u. Schur's
lemma makes h_u exactly scalar on it, with energy alpha_u. The first
unrestricted energy outside the vacuum and A_u is beta_u, and

```text
alpha_u/sqrt(u) -> sqrt(3),
beta_u/sqrt(u) -> sqrt(5).                                (15)
```

For SU(N) the complex adjoint is irreducible and self-dual, and its
invariant bilinear form is unique up to scale. Thus
dim(A_u tensor A_u)^G=1. Choose the real equivariant frame if writing
the singlet as d^-1/2 sum_a e_a tensor e_a. All eigenvalue and
representation statements here hold on the full local Hilbert space;
the common Gauss constraint is imposed only after taking copies.

## 4. Literal approximation of the entire slow adjoint

In global coordinates U=U1 U2, K=U1, define the true-vacuum marginal and
full, not locally invariant, source map

```text
mu_u(U)=integral |Omega_u(U,K)|^2 dK,
J_u f=f(U)Omega_u,    J_u:L2(G,mu_u dU)->L2(G^2),
P_u=J_u J_u*,
P_u psi=Omega_u mu_u^-1 integral Omega_u psi dK.             (16)
```

For fixed u these are well-defined smooth objects, J_u is an equivariant
isometry, P_u Omega_u=Omega_u exactly, and P_u preserves H1. Only its
fixed-u positivity and smoothness are used for domains. No lower bound
on mu_u uniform in u is required.

Put g=u^-1/4 and use the local balanced chart

```text
U=exp(sqrt(2)g q),     K=U^(1/2)exp(g z/sqrt(2)).
```

Choose the equivariant unitary identification of A_u with its limiting
linear q Hermite space from Section 3. Every fixed basis vector obeys

```text
psi_a,u -> c_q q_a Omega_0,
Omega_u -> Omega_0=Phi_q Phi_z                           (17)
```

in the flattened local L2 coordinates, with vanishing chart tails.
c_q normalizes each linear Hermite function.

For a fixed large R, the coarse vector-valued function

```text
f_a,u,R(U)=c_q chi(|q|/R)q_a
```

is defined in the chart and extended smoothly by zero. Its components
transform as the adjoint and have a bound independent of u for fixed R.
Multiplication commutes with the local Haar flattening. The triangle
inequality and (17) give

```text
limsup_(u->infinity)||psi_a,u-f_a,u,R Omega_u||
 <= ||c_q(1-chi(|q|/R))q_a Omega_0||.                       (18)
```

The right side tends to zero as R tends to infinity. This order of limits
avoids multiplying an L2 error by an unbounded polynomial. There are only
d basis vectors for fixed N, so (18) proves operator-norm leakage

```text
d_A(u)=||(I-P_u)E_A,u|| -> 0.                              (19)
```

Equivariance additionally yields the exact scalar identity (6).
The same bounded-cutoff argument with the radial q polynomial, already
proved in the companion note, gives d_r(u)->0. No full higher-quadratic
irrep source frame is needed: Section 2 excludes those representations
from this common-Gauss low window.

The products of the coarse adjoint functions, contracted with the
invariant bilinear form, are concrete joint coarse sources for s_ij.
They are invariant under the one common group. Requiring each f_a to be
a class function would remove these sources and would be the wrong
source algebra for the common-Gauss problem.

## 5. The actual additive common-Gauss conclusion

For M copies impose one simultaneous conjugation action on all 2M
holonomies. The true ground remains exactly Omega_u^tensor M: each
summand is nonnegative after E0 subtraction, and their product ground
is invariant. The physical Hamiltonian is the restriction of sum_i h_u,i
to the jointly invariant Hilbert space.

The source domain and projection are

```text
J_M: L2(G^M,mu_u^tensor M)^G -> (tensor_i L2(G^2))^G,
J_M f=f(U_1,...,U_M) Omega_u^tensor M,
P_M=(tensor_i P_u)|_(common G invariants).                  (20)
```

These are actual literal jointly invariant coarse observables applied
to the true ground. Let

```text
t_u=min{b_u,alpha_u+beta_u,3alpha_u},
f_u=t_u-max{(t_u-a_u)d_r(u)^2,
            (t_u-2alpha_u)[2d_A(u)^2-d_A(u)^4]}.            (21)
```

Equations (13),(15) show that max(a_u,2alpha_u)<t_u for all sufficiently
large u, with the threshold independent of M. The full form compression
to the complement of (20), after subtracting the true full product-vacuum
energy M E0(u), satisfies

```text
F_M,u >= f_u I,            for every finite M>=1,
f_u/sqrt(u) -> sqrt(3)+sqrt(5).                            (22)
```

For M=1 the unused pair term in (21) only weakens the valid bound. Thus

```text
liminf_(u->infinity) inf_(M>=1)
  [inf spectrum(F_M,u)/sqrt(u)] >= sqrt(3)+sqrt(5).          (23)
```

The complete low projection Pi_M,u below t_u has the rank and basis (5).
Its literal source frame has the exact weights (8), uniformly in M,
and is onto this whole physical window with a right inverse bounded by
min{1-d_r(u)^2,(1-d_A(u)^2)^2}^-1/2. Arbitrary entangled superpositions
of the pair singlets are included.

For every finite M, the form restriction is densely defined and closed:
P_M preserves H1 at fixed u,M, and the full form is elliptic on the
compact product. No bound on the H1 norm of P_M uniform in M is used in
the spectral estimate.

For countably many copies, work in the incomplete tensor product based
on the exact local vacuum. The simultaneous compact-group action is
strongly continuous on the finite-excitation core and hence on its
completion. Define h_infinity by the nonnegative tensor spectral sum;
its invariant part is reducing. The literal infinite tensor projection
preserves every finite excitation-support space. Applying its complement
to finite smooth-excitation vectors gives a form-domain set dense in its
Hilbert-space range. Restricting the closed full form therefore defines a closed
complementary form, and (21)--(23) and the onto frame pass to the l2
sum of the one-block and pair-block sectors. Compact resolvent is not
asserted for this infinite model.

## 6. What this resolves, and what it does not

The common Gauss constraint creates cross-block adjoint singlets below
the mixed local threshold. It does not create a copy-count loss in the
literal projection estimate. Exact vacuum inclusion makes distinct
excitation supports orthogonal under the projection error; Schur's
lemma controls the whole local adjoint, and the complete spectral
classification limits the number of excited blocks to two.

The new conclusion is a uniform lower floor and a complete literal
low-window source frame for this additive common-Gauss model. It is
stronger than tensorizing already invariant single-block Hilbert spaces.
No equality for the literal complementary floor is claimed, and the
normalization is an excitation energy of the actual additive Hamiltonian,
not a gap inside its compressed fast operator.

Ambient plaquettes or shared electric derivatives between different
blocks change both the true vacuum and the exact excitation-support
decomposition. The next interacting obligation is to control the
true-vacuum literal projection against the interacting low spectral
space by a local excitation or relative-form estimate that replaces
(2) and the additive classification. The current result supplies the
uncoupled common-Gauss reference, including its charged intermediate
representations, for that comparison. It does not establish a uniform
Schur lift under interactions, a Wilson identification of the normalized
coarse operator, an OS-history identification, or a continuum mass.

The finite rational controls in
[check_common_gauss_literal_projection.py](../../runs/literal_quantum_sources_2026-09-05/check_common_gauss_literal_projection.py)
test (7)--(10) using an equivariant vacuum/radial/adjoint model, direct
tensor-support expansions, and an exact-vacuum negative control. They
do not certify the compact Wilson localization or the large-u limit.
