# WORKHOUSE-related research paper library

Collected and verified on 2026-08-21. This is a paper-only research library mapped to the scientific subjects present in WORKHOUSE. It does not contain a repository review or improvement recommendations.

## What is here

- **99 open-access PDFs**, all parsed successfully and SHA-256 pinned (99.0 MiB).
- **59 core papers**, **23 emerging papers**, and **17 frontier/watchlist papers**.
- **30 papers from 2024–2026** and **26 papers with at least 100 citations** in the recorded source.
- **84 papers with a verified journal/proceedings or DOI record** in the collected metadata.
- **42 additional classic or publisher-only papers** in `LINKS_ONLY_CLASSICS.md`.
- Coverage spans 1983–2026 for downloaded papers; the links-only foundations extend back to 1958.

## How the folders are organized

`papers/` is divided first by evidence maturity and then by topic:

- `core_cited_and_canonical/`: established benchmarks, widely cited methods, and exact-fit foundations.
- `emerging_peer_reviewed_and_direct/`: recent papers with publication review and/or early citation uptake.
- `frontier_watchlist/`: the newest work, separated because citation counts are not mature; this includes both new journal papers and preprints.

Topic coverage:

- Glueball spectrum, continuum limits, and large N: 27 PDFs
- Flux tubes, torelons, string tension, and effective strings: 10 PDFs
- Spectral extraction, operators, overlaps, and error reduction: 9 PDFs
- Rigorous lattice Yang–Mills, mass gap, loop equations, and surfaces: 19 PDFs
- Haar integration and Weingarten calculus: 8 PDFs
- Effective Hamiltonians, Schrieffer–Wolff, and Feshbach methods: 5 PDFs
- Hamiltonian and gauge-invariant formulations: 11 PDFs
- Strong-coupling and linked-cluster expansions: 5 PDFs
- Flat bands, line graphs, and homological topology: 5 PDFs
