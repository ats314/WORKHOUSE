# WORKHOUSE-related research paper library — Volume 2

Collected and verified on 2026-08-21. This is a second, strictly non-overlapping paper collection discovered primarily by following backward and forward citation trails from the strongest Volume 1 anchors. It contains papers only—no repository review, critique, or improvement suggestions.

## What is here

- **161 new open-access PDFs**, all parsed successfully and SHA-256 pinned (212.9 MiB; 5,091 pages).
- **80 core papers**, **59 emerging papers**, and **22 frontier/watchlist papers**.
- **40 papers from 2024–2026** and **24 papers with at least 100 citations** in the recorded source.
- **137 papers with a verified journal/proceedings or DOI record** in the collected metadata.
- **8 additional non-arXiv classics** in `LINKS_ONLY_CLASSICS.md`.
- **Zero overlap** by arXiv ID, normalized title, or DOI with all 99 Volume 1 PDFs and its 42 links-only records.
- Downloaded-paper coverage spans 1992–2026.

## Organization

`papers/` is divided first by evidence maturity and then by topic:

- `core_cited_and_canonical/`: established benchmarks, highly cited methods, and exact-fit foundations.
- `emerging_peer_reviewed_and_direct/`: recent direct papers with publication review and/or fast early uptake.
- `frontier_watchlist/`: the newest work, kept separate because citation counts are not mature.

Topic coverage:

- Glueball spectrum, continuum limits, finite volume, and large N: 25 PDFs
- Flux tubes, torelons, string tension, and effective strings: 36 PDFs
- Spectral extraction, operators, overlaps, and inverse methods: 10 PDFs
- Rigorous lattice Yang–Mills, correlation decay, loop equations, and surfaces: 21 PDFs
- Haar integration, Weingarten calculus, and matrix-group surfaces: 9 PDFs
- Effective Hamiltonians, perturbative gadgets, Schrieffer–Wolff, and Feshbach methods: 10 PDFs
- Hamiltonian and gauge-invariant lattice formulations: 41 PDFs
- Flat bands, compact localized states, and graph topology: 9 PDFs
