# Blockwise conditional Poincaré under the Wilson Gibbs measure (referee version)

*Status:* **proof-level**, finite-cutoff (compact state space), conditional on coarse data.  
*Theme:* turning **local curvature + Lyapunov drift** into a **uniform blockwise Poincaré inequality**
with the correct $g(a)^2$ scaling.

---

## 1. Setting

Let $G$ be a compact Lie group (e.g. $SU(N)$). Fix a lattice spacing $a>0$ and a single $2a$-block $B$.
The fine configuration space is a compact manifold
\[
\Omega_B^{\mathrm{fine}} \;\cong\; G^N,
\]
with $N$ fixed (independent of $a$). Let $\mathcal F_{2a}$ be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by coarse
($2a$-scale) holonomies (the “boundary data” for the block).

Define the **conditional Wilson Gibbs measure**
\[
d\mu_{a,B}(\,\cdot\,\mid \mathcal F_{2a})
\;=\;
Z^{-1}\exp\!\Big(-\frac{1}{g(a)^2}S_{a,B}(\,\cdot\,;\text{coarse})\Big)\,d\mathrm{vol},
\]
where $S_{a,B}$ is the Wilson action restricted to plaquettes intersecting $B$.

Let $V:=S_{a,B}/g(a)^2$.
Consider the **reversible Langevin generator**
\[
L_{a,B}^{\mathrm{fine}}
\;:=\;
\Delta_{\mathrm{fine}} - \nabla V\cdot\nabla
\;=\;
\Delta_{\mathrm{fine}} - \frac{1}{g(a)^2}\nabla S_{a,B}\cdot\nabla,
\]
acting on gauge-invariant (horizontal) functions. Its Dirichlet form is
\[
\mathcal E_{a,B}^{\mathrm{fine}}(\phi,\phi)
\;=\;
\int_{\Omega_B^{\mathrm{fine}}} |\nabla_{\mathrm{fine}}\phi|^2\,d\mu_{a,B}(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{2a}).
\]

---

## 2. Hypotheses (uniform in coarse data)

### (H1) Local convexity on a small-field region
There exists a canonical small-field set $K_B\subset \Omega_B^{\mathrm{fine}}$ and $\kappa>0$ such that
\[
\nabla^2 S_{a,B}(U)\;\succeq\;\kappa\,\Pi_{\mathrm{hor}}
\qquad\forall U\in K_B,
\]
uniformly in $a$, the block $B$, and the coarse configuration.

### (H2) Lyapunov drift outside $K_B$
There exists $W\in C^2(\Omega_B^{\mathrm{fine}})$ with $W\ge 1$ and constants $c_1,c_2>0$ such that
\[
L_{a,B}^{\mathrm{fine}}W
\;\le\;
-c_1\,W + c_2\,\mathbf 1_{K_B},
\]
uniformly in $a,B$, and coarse configuration.

### (H3) Gauge degeneracy handled
Vertical directions lie in the kernel of the Dirichlet form. We work on the horizontal sector and
with centered observables.

---

## 3. Lemma (blockwise conditional Poincaré)

**Claim.** There exists $\lambda_{\mathrm{block}}>0$, independent of $a$, $B$, and coarse data, such that
for every smooth horizontal $\phi$ with $\mathbb E_{\mu_{a,B}}[\phi\mid\mathcal F_{2a}]=0$,
\[
\mathrm{Var}_{\mu_{a,B}(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{2a})}(\phi)
\;\le\;
\frac{g(a)^2}{\lambda_{\mathrm{block}}}\;
\int_{\Omega_B^{\mathrm{fine}}}|\nabla_{\mathrm{fine}}\phi|^2\,d\mu_{a,B}(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{2a}).
\tag{BP}
\]

Equivalently,
\[
\mathrm{gap}\!\left(L_{a,B}^{\mathrm{fine}}\right)
\;\ge\;
\frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{block}}}{g(a)^2}
\quad\text{on the horizontal sector.}
\]

---

## 4. Proof

### Step 1. Local Bakry–Émery curvature lower bound on $K_B$
On the compact manifold $\Omega_B^{\mathrm{fine}}\cong G^N$, the Ricci curvature has a uniform lower bound
$\mathrm{Ric}\succeq -C_0\,\mathrm{Id}$ for some $C_0=C_0(G,N)$.

On $K_B$, hypothesis (H1) implies
\[
\nabla^2 V
=
\frac{1}{g(a)^2}\nabla^2 S_{a,B}
\;\succeq\;
\frac{\kappa}{g(a)^2}\,\Pi_{\mathrm{hor}}.
\]
Hence on $K_B$,
\[
\mathrm{Ric}+\nabla^2 V
\;\succeq\;
\Big(\frac{\kappa}{g(a)^2}-C_0\Big)\Pi_{\mathrm{hor}}
\;\succeq\;
\frac{\kappa_1}{g(a)^2}\Pi_{\mathrm{hor}},
\]
for some $\kappa_1>0$ once $g(a)^2$ is sufficiently small (or by absorbing constants into $\kappa_1$).
Thus a local $CD(\kappa_1/g(a)^2,\infty)$ bound holds on $K_B$ for horizontal observables.

### Step 2. Patch to a global Poincaré inequality using Lyapunov drift
By (H2), $L_{a,B}^{\mathrm{fine}}$ admits a Lyapunov function returning to $K_B$ with uniform constants.
A standard localization theorem (Cattiaux–Guillin / Bakry–Gentil–Ledoux type) implies:

> local Poincaré/curvature control on $K_B$ + Lyapunov drift outside $K_B$
> $\Rightarrow$ global Poincaré inequality with constant comparable to the local one,
> with constants depending only on $(\kappa_1,c_1,c_2)$.

Since the local curvature scale is $\kappa_1/g(a)^2$, the resulting global Poincaré constant is $O(g(a)^2)$,
uniformly in the coarse configuration.

### Step 3. Horizontality and centering
Vertical modes lie in $\ker\mathcal E_{a,B}^{\mathrm{fine}}$, and centering removes constants. Also
$|\nabla_{\mathrm{hor}}\phi|\le|\nabla_{\mathrm{fine}}\phi|$. Hence (BP) holds as stated. $\square$

---

## 5. Why this is the right block inequality

- The $g(a)^2$ factor is forced by the scaling of $V=S/g(a)^2$.
- Conditioning on $\mathcal F_{2a}$ is what eliminates IR/volume dependence.
- The only genuinely nontrivial inputs are (H1) and (H2) *uniform in coarse data*.
