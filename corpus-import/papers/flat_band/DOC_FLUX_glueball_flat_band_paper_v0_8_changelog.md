# Paper v0.8 changelog

Relative to v0.7:

1. Replaced the unreduced canonical statement
   `B_N=P_402(N)/D_409(N)` by the exact reduced form
   `B_N=P_17(N^2)/(N R_20(N^2))`.
2. Added the large-N consequences
   `q_N=-227/N^5+O(N^-7)`, `A_N=640/N^7+O(N^-9)`,
   `B_N=6170/(9N^7)+O(N^-9)`, and
   `Delta c_4,N=11930/(9N^7)+O(N^-9)`.
3. Added the exceptional-rank determinant analysis:
   - SU(4): `Delta A_4=Delta B_4=0`;
   - SU(5): no determinant assignment occurs;
   - SU(6): the sole determinant orbit is absent from the A/B target.
4. Promoted the fourth-order band-shape theorem from N>=7 to every integer
   N>=3. SU(2) remains separate.
5. Narrowed the open rank-specific work to q_4, q_6, and SU(2).
6. Added explicit compact polynomials P_17 and R_20 and updated the
   verification appendix.

The SU(3) theorem, real-space SOS identity, exact kernel, and lower-order
coefficients are unchanged.
