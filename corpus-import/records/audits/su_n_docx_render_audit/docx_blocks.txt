P010 [heading 1] Abstract
P011 [First Paragraph] This manuscript develops a larger theory from two bodies of supplied work: the compact one-plaquette class Hamiltonian at large potential strength and the strong-coupling one-plaquette glueball band on the three-dimensional cubic lattice. Its new proved core is a fixed-rank $SU(N)$ extension of the charge-odd flat-band mechanism. At second order in the magnetic coupling, the orientation-sensitive hopping coefficient is
P012 [Body Text] $(t)_(N)=((2N((N)^(2)−4))/(((N)^(2)−1)(2(N)^(2)−1)(4(N)^(2)−9))),  N≥3.$
P013 [First Paragraph] It is positive, reduces to $5/612$ at $N=3$, and obeys $(N)^(3)(t)_(N)→1/4$. The spatial matrix multiplying $(t)_(N)$ is the signed face-adjacency operator

P015 [First Paragraph] where $over(N,~)$ is the Bloch face-to-edge boundary matrix. Because $(∂)_(2)(∂)_(3)=0$, $S()$ has an exactly flat eigenvalue $−4$ for every momentum. In fact its full spectrum is
P016 [Body Text] $specS()={−4, q()−4, q()−4},  q()=4underover(∑,j=1,3)(sin)^(2)​(((k)_(j))/(2)).$
P017 [First Paragraph] Consequently the lowest $C$-odd branch is dispersionless through $O((y)^(2))$ for every $SU(N≥3)$, the full one-plaquette $C$-odd manifold has width

P019 [First Paragraph] and $(W)_(N)^((−))∼3(y)^(2)/(N)^(3)$ at fixed strong-coupling normalization. On an $(L)^(3)$ torus the flat eigenspace has exact dimension $(L)^(3)+2$.
P020 [Body Text] The second half of the manuscript organizes the supplied local spectral results into a “Weyl–Hodge” program. The internal axis is controlled by Weyl-invariant degree and yields the exact $SU(3)$ three-term local gap. P113 [Body Text] $(over(N,~))^(†)over(N,~)∼qI−u (u)^(†),  q=underover(∑,j=1,3)(|(u)_(j)|)^(2).$

P219 [heading 1] 8. The second rank-cubic scale
P220 [heading 2] 8.1 Why the fixed-rank expansion requires $β≫(N)^(3)$
P221 [First Paragraph] In either local class gap, the displayed $(β)^(−1/2)$ coefficient grows like $(N)^(5/2)$, while the leading oscillator gap grows like $sqrt(β/N)$. Their relative size is therefore
P222 [Body Text] $(((N)^(5/2)(β)^(−1/2))/(sqrt(β/N)))≍(((N)^(3))/(β)).$
P223 [First Paragraph] Thus the fixed-rank series is asymptotically ordered only when
P224 [Body Text] $β≫(N)^(3).$
P225 [First Paragraph] A formal ’t Hooft substitution $β∝(N)^(2)$ lies outside this uniformity regime and cannot be used to infer a controlled large-$N$ local gap.
P226 [heading 2] 8.2 Formal double scaling at $β=(N)^(3)τ$
P227 [First Paragraph] If one nevertheless substitutes
P228 [Body Text] $β=(N)^(3)τ$
P229 [First Paragraph] into the displayed coefficients and divides by $N$, the formal outer expressions become
P230 [Body Text] $(((Δ)_(N)^(+)((N)^(3)τ))/(N))∼sqrt(2τ)−((1)/(8))−((3sqrt(2))/(512sqrt(τ)))+⋯,$
P231 [First Paragraph] $(((Δ)_(N)^(−)((N)^(3)τ))/(N))∼3sqrt(((τ)/(2)))−((3)/(16))−((7sqrt(2))/(768sqrt(τ)))+⋯.$
P232 [First Paragraph] These are conjectural scaling targets, not asymptotic theorems, because the $N$-dependence of the $(O)_(N)((β)^(−1))$ remainder is uncontrolled.
P233 [Body Text] The correct next theorem would be a uniform estimate of the form
P234 [Body Text] $|(((Δ)_(N)^(±)((N)^(3)τ))/(N))−(F)_(±)^((K))(τ)|≤(ε)_(N,K)(τ),$
P235 [First Paragraph] with a domain $τ≥(τ)_(0)>0$ and $(ε)_(N,K)→0$ as $N→∞$. No such estimate is presently established.
