**Lemma (Local Hodge stiffness from exactness + gauge term).**
Let (d_0,d_1,d_2) be the cochain coboundaries on a cubical complex with (d_1 d_0=0) and (d_2 d_1=0). Let (\Delta_1=d_1^\top d_1 + d_0 d_0^\top). Then for any (x\in C^1),
[
\langle x,\Delta_1 x\rangle=|d_1 x|^2+|d_0^\top x|^2,
]
so (\Delta_1\succeq 0) and (\ker\Delta_1={x:\ d_1 x=0,\ d_0^\top x=0}\cong H^1.)
In particular, on the orthogonal complement of harmonic 1-forms, (\Delta_1) has a strictly positive spectral gap; hence (m^2 I+\alpha\Delta_1) has minimum eigenvalue (m^2) and an exponentially decaying inverse kernel.

That’s it. No “redundancy creates curvature” rhetoric needed; the algebra already gives you stiffness once you add the gauge term and (optionally) a mass.
