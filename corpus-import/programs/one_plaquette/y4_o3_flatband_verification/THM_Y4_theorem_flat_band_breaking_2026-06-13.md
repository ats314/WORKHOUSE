# Fourth-Order Breakdown of the SU(3) One-Flux C-Odd Flat Band

**Date:** June 13, 2026  
**Status:** Exact finite-order theorem with machine-checkable rational certificates  
**Scope:** Kogut-Susskind Hamiltonian, cubic spatial lattice, SU(3), normalized one-flux C-odd sector, des Cloizeaux effective Hamiltonian through fourth order in the strong-coupling variable \(y=2\beta/3\).

## Theorem

Let \(H_{\mathrm{eff},-}(y)\) denote the C-odd one-flux effective Hamiltonian,

\[
H_{\mathrm{eff},-}(y)
=\frac83 I+yI+y^2H_2+y^3H_3+y^4H_4+O(y^5).
\]

The lowest \(T_1^{+-}\) branch is exactly flat through third order,

\[
m_-(k,y)=\frac83+y+\frac{11}{306}y^2
-\frac{109151}{249696}y^3+O(y^4),
\]

but is not flat at fourth order. More precisely:

1. The exact fourth-order operator \(H_4\) is a finite-range, translation-invariant, cubic-covariant, Hermitian operator on oriented plaquettes.
2. For the consistently oriented boundary \(\psi_c\) of an elementary cube,

\[
H_4\psi_c=c_4\psi_c+r_c,
\]

where

\[
c_4=-\frac{4555981615057344457}{1812647572150615200},
\qquad r_c\neq0.
\]

The residual \(r_c\) has support on 30 plaquettes outside the rigid six-face component, and

\[
\|r_c\|_{\infty}=\frac5{48}.
\]

Exactly six residual plaquettes attain magnitude \(5/48\).
3. Consequently,

\[
[H_4,P_{\mathrm{flat}}]\neq0,
\]

so the cube-boundary flat subspace is not invariant at fourth order.
4. The fourth-order correction to the former flat branch differs at the high-symmetry momenta \((\pi,0,0)\) and \((\pi,\pi,\pi)\):

\[
c_4(\pi,0,0)
=-\frac{17700498622147435111}{7250590288602460800},
\]

\[
c_4(\pi,\pi,\pi)
=-\frac{3447362930970494909}{1450118057720492160},
\]

and hence

\[
c_4(\pi,\pi,\pi)-c_4(\pi,0,0)
=\frac{17607806155349}{275331901291200}
\approx0.06395120243159332>0.
\]

Therefore the first nonzero bandwidth occurs at order \(y^4\). In particular, if \(W_-(y)\) is the bandwidth of the branch that was flat through \(O(y^3)\), then

\[
W_-(y)=W_4y^4+O(y^5),
\qquad
W_4\ge
\frac{17607806155349}{275331901291200}>0.
\]

This theorem makes no all-orders or continuum claim.

## Proof

### 1. Fourth-order process space

All rooted, site-connected multisets of four plaquette insertions were enumerated, allowing repeated insertions. Quotienting by the proper cubic stabilizer of the rooted input plaquette gives

\[
182{,}440
\]

connected support classes. Attaching every one-plaquette output compatible with the geometric support gives

\[
895{,}524
\]

support/output candidates. Exact linkwise SU(3) triality leaves 449 support/output classes, whose ordered insertion words reduce to

\[
4{,}221
\]

rooted symmetry classes.

Triality is only necessary. Exact singlet-channel fusion removes the residual false positives and produces 16,835 valid charge-conjugation orientation orbits.

### 2. Exact local Haar and representation data

Every surviving link integral belongs to one of five local moment families,

\[
(1,1),\quad(2,2),\quad(3,0),\quad(3,3),\quad(6,0),
\]

with invariant-space dimensions

\[
1,\quad2,\quad1,\quad6,\quad5.
\]

The exact rational Haar projectors were constructed from invariant Gram matrices. The balanced moments reproduce

\[
\operatorname{Wg}_1=\frac13,
\qquad
\operatorname{Wg}_2(e)=\frac18,
\qquad
\operatorname{Wg}_2((12))=-\frac1{24},
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Wg}_3(e)=\frac7{120},
\qquad
\operatorname{Wg}_3(\text{transposition})=-\frac1{40},
\qquad
\operatorname{Wg}_3(\text{3-cycle})=\frac1{60}.
\]

The unbalanced degree-three and degree-six moments are represented by exact epsilon and double-epsilon projectors.

### 3. Time-resolved fusion channels

The final Haar projector alone is insufficient because the resolvent denominators depend on the intermediate SU(3) representations. Each local token signature was therefore resolved into exact fusion-tree paths. The 182 signatures yield 380 complete local paths, generated by 30 nonzero multiplicity-free SU(3) intertwiners. Each intertwiner reproduces, exactly, the corresponding Casimir spectral projector

\[
P_T
=J_T(J_T^{\mathsf T}GJ_T)^{-1}J_T^{\mathsf T}G.
\]

For every token signature, the resulting path tensors are mutually orthogonal and span exactly the same invariant subspace as the independent Haar basis. Thus the denominator-resolved decomposition is complete rather than an approximation.

### 4. Fourth-order des Cloizeaux operator

Let

\[
R=Q(E_0-H_0)^{-1}Q,
\qquad PVP=aP
\]

in the fixed charge sector. Define

\[
D_4=PVRVRVRVP,
\]

\[
E_L=PVR^2VRVP,
\qquad
E_R=PVRVR^2VP,
\]

\[
F=PVR^3VP,
\qquad
B=PVRVP,
\qquad
D_2=PVR^2VP.
\]

The fourth-order Hermitian des Cloizeaux coefficient is

\[
H_4
=D_4-a(E_L+E_R)+a^2F-\frac12\{B,D_2\}.
\]

The ordered-path coefficients implied by this formula were independently checked against exact fourth-order eigenvalue series for three finite rational Hamiltonians. All three regressions agree coefficient by coefficient.

The complete calculation contains

\[
3{,}895
\]

trace-wiring topologies and

\[
35{,}979
\]

global fusion-tree paths. They split into 6,598 nonresonant-only, 2,749 mixed, and 7,488 all-resonant orientation orbits. Direct and folded/subtraction terms were evaluated in exact rational arithmetic. The 1,478 nonresonant topologies independently reproduce the prior nonresonant contraction results.

### 5. Real-space fourth-order kernel

After exact C-odd orientation signs and rooted cubic multiplicities are applied, the result is a translation-invariant real-space kernel

\[
T_4^{\alpha\beta}(r)\in\mathbb Q,
\]

with 189 nonzero entries. It obeys exact Hermiticity,

\[
T_4^{\alpha\beta}(r)
=T_4^{\beta\alpha}(-r),
\]

and proper cubic covariance.

Let the elementary cube state at the origin be

\[
\psi_c
=(\tau_x-1)p_{yz}-(\tau_y-1)p_{xz}+(\tau_z-1)p_{xy}.
\]

Direct application of the 189-entry kernel gives support on 36 plaquettes. On the original six faces, all coefficient ratios agree and define

\[
c_4=-\frac{4555981615057344457}{1812647572150615200}.
\]

Subtracting \(c_4\psi_c\) leaves 30 nonzero plaquettes. Six have coefficients

\[
\pm\frac5{48},
\]

so the residual is exactly nonzero. Hence \(H_4\psi_c\notin\operatorname{span}\{\psi_c\}\), and the flat subspace generated by translated cube boundaries is not invariant under \(H_4\).

### 6. Independent momentum witness

The Bloch symbol

\[
H_4(k)=\sum_r T_4(r)e^{ik\cdot r}
\]

was evaluated at parity momenta. Contracting with the unperturbed flat vector gives the exact corrections quoted in the theorem. Their positive difference

\[
\frac{17607806155349}{275331901291200}
\]

is an independent witness that the fourth-order correction is momentum dependent.

Since the branch is exactly flat through \(O(y^3)\), this establishes that its first nonzero bandwidth occurs at \(O(y^4)\). ∎

## Scope boundary

Proved:

- the one-flux C-odd band is flat through \(O(y^3)\);
- its fourth-order effective Hamiltonian does not preserve the compact cube-boundary subspace;
- the fourth-order correction is momentum dependent;
- the first nonzero strong-coupling bandwidth occurs at \(O(y^4)\).

Not proved:

- a closed form for the full fourth-order bandwidth maximum over all momenta;
- behavior at fifth and higher orders;
- convergence of the strong-coupling series;
- any continuum-limit glueball dispersion or Yang-Mills mass-gap statement.
