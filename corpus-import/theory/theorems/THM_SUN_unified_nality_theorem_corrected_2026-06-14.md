## Unified local invariant principle

For a fourth-order matrix element, each link carries at most six fundamental/antifundamental factors:

\[
r_\ell+s_\ell\le 6.
\]

A local SU(N) invariant can occur only when

\[
r_\ell-s_\ell\equiv0\pmod N.
\]

The corresponding Haar projector is the projector onto the full invariant space in

\[
V^{\otimes r_\ell}\otimes \bar V^{\otimes s_\ell}.
\]

Its balanced part is represented by walled-Brauer pairings. Nonzero N-ality sectors are determinant-dressed invariant spaces generated by epsilon tensors and pairings, with all finite-rank linear dependencies removed by the exact Gram quotient.

This gives one rank-unified contraction rule:

\[

where \(\mathcal W_N\) is evaluated with the complete local Haar projector.

The rank selector may be written as

\[

\]
