The full mathematical review of WORKHOUSE is **not complete**. The September 7
audit inventories the wider `C:/WORKHOUSE` tree and nested archives, records
extraction and review coverage, and preserves the pending sources in the theory
graph. A digest, extracted text, graph node or folder called `ALREADY_REVIEWED`
does not mean that every claim has been examined.

census, exclusions, archive-member provenance, coverage contract, bounded

[`WORKHOUSE_FULL_2026-09-07` inventory](../notes/WORKHOUSE_FULL_2026-09-07.jsonl)

remain local; external papers and configuration contents are not republished.

PDF text is not a visual or equation audit. Image dimensions, numeric-array
headers and pickle opcode inspection identify evidence without interpreting
every image or array or executing serialized code. Unsupported scientific
binaries and inaccessible historical sources remain explicit. The run contains
the exact final representation counts.

## Why earlier completeness language was insufficient

ADR 0016's “whole corpus” meant the specifically pinned `corpus-import` snapshot,
not every root folder or later archive. The March organization documents describe
different historical scopes. Their “zero content excluded” claim coexists with
exclusions of conversation JSON/HTML and protobuf exports for size or format.
Those reasons do not establish absence of unique research.

Historical source locations on other drives, unavailable exports and earlier
Git history are outside the observed snapshot unless exact local copies were
recovered. This audit does not claim to have recovered every historical version.

smooth-proxy weighting mechanism that removes a naked volume term, a correctly

candidates with explicit certificate premises. They also locate specific failed
steps in older RG, scaling-limit and functional-inequality arguments and state

blanket verdicts on whole documents.

Five recovered Lean files were read in full. Independent integer arithmetic
checks their finite denominator data; no current-toolchain Lean build or T0

C2's September resolution, the symbolic all-rank assembly and fixed-spacing G18
remain established at their registered scopes. The bounded reads did not find
a theorem already supplying the interacting G19 comparison. This statement
concerns the sections actually read; the pending corpus is not exhausted.

Further review follows the actual missing mechanisms: harmonic dynamics,
uniform interacting form/score remainders, moving sources and high retained

connection must name the hypotheses supplied and the mathematical step it enables.
Old coefficient-signature ranking alone is insufficient for this analysis archive.

## Quarantine and completion

The maintainer authorized moving useless or redundant documents without deleting
them. Exactly 36 confirmed duplicates were moved to
`C:/WORKHOUSE/quarantine/2026-09-07-exact-duplicates`, with SHA-256 verification
of every moved file and an identical retained copy. The run preserves the path
map and completed move journal. The loose `organized_pdfs` and `organized_proofs`
collections have redirect notes; four same-folder ` - Copy.tex` files were also
moved. No repository, unique source, program or sealed evidence was moved.
Useful failed arguments and distinct `SYNTH_COPY` notes remain available.

Inventory completeness is checked against the pinned census and archive inputs,
not just the selected output manifest. Semantic review is complete only when
every substantive unique source has a recorded review, a verified duplicate link
to sufficiently reviewed content, or a justified exclusion. Partial reads keep
their unread remainder pending. Quarantine is a documented location, not a
mathematical verdict.
