## Why earlier completeness language was insufficient

ADR 0016's “whole corpus” meant the specifically pinned `corpus-import` snapshot,
not every root folder or later archive. The March organization documents describe
different historical scopes. Their “zero content excluded” claim coexists with
exclusions of conversation JSON/HTML and protobuf exports for size or format.
Those reasons do not establish absence of unique research.

Historical source locations on other drives, unavailable exports and earlier
Git history are outside the observed snapshot unless exact local copies were
recovered. This audit does not claim to have recovered every historical version.

mathematical verdict.
