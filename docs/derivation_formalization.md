## Read a proof and its dependencies

1. Use the statement ID in [the source-pinned inventory](../ledger/derivation_statements.yaml). Each record has the exact source SHA-256, section/line locator, hypotheses, source dependencies and remaining formalization.
2. `workhouse why DERIV:WILSON_SC17_THERMODYNAMIC_LIMIT:IF4_CLOSABLE` shows the source claim and its scoped Lean support. `formalizes` means the whole named statement; `supported_by` means the stated ingredient only.
3. `workhouse why LEAN:closed_extension_of_integration_by_parts` shows the compiled declaration and its actual project proof dependencies. The [kernel export](../ledger/lean_dependencies.json) also includes every directly used mathlib constant and transitive axiom. Local definitions are traversed until the next registered theorem; mathematical source dependencies are curated separately.

The kernel export currently covers 439 registered theorems. It is rejected if a Lean source, import root or toolchain pin changes. Lean fingerprints normalize line endings only (`utf8-lf`); derivation hashes preserve the original bytes. Dependency extraction does not prove that a source theorem has been faithfully encoded; the precise scoped mapping records that review.
