\subsection{Physical-time blocking of the actual Wilson transfer}
\label{sec:wdt-blocked-vacuum}

Retain the fundamental-character clock $\tau=\tau_F(\epsilon)$ and the
retuned Wilson transfer
\[
 T_{\epsilon,L}(u)=e^{\tau uV_L/2}e^{-\tau K_{\epsilon,L}}e^{\tau uV_L/2},
 \qquad \beta_t=2N/\epsilon,\qquad \beta_s=2Nu\tau.
\]
The preceding uniform kinetic-window result supplies
$k_\epsilon(F)=C_F/2$, a uniform one-link gap $\gamma=C_F/2$,
$k_\epsilon(R)\to C_R/2$ at every fixed representation, and
$p_\epsilon\ge a h_{\epsilon/4}$ for one $a>0$. Here $h_s$ is the
heat kernel of $e^{-sC_2/2}$ and $D=N^2-1$.
These are inputs, not consequences of a finite representation scan.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform smoothing and coarse mixing]
\label{lem:wdt-smoothing}
There is $A<\infty$ such that
\[
 \|p_\epsilon^{*m}\|_\infty\le A[1+(m\tau)^{-D/2}],\qquad m\ge1.
\]
For any $\delta>0$ there is a finite $s_0$, independent of the temporal
mesh, such that with
\[
 m_\epsilon=\lceil s_0/\tau\rceil,\quad
 s_\epsilon=m_\epsilon\tau\in[s_0,s_0+\tau_0],
\]
one has $\|p_\epsilon^{*m_\epsilon}-1\|_\infty\le\delta$ for all
sufficiently small $\epsilon$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $p_\epsilon=a h_{\epsilon/4}+(1-a)r_\epsilon$ with
$r_\epsilon$ a probability density. In the binomial expansion of the
convolution power, terms with at least $am/2$ heat factors are bounded by
$C[1+(m\epsilon)^{-D/2}]$. The remaining binomial probability is at most
$e^{-am/8}$, while its density is bounded by $C\epsilon^{-D/2}$.
The finite supremum of $x^{D/2}e^{-ax/8}$ absorbs this term. The clock
and $\epsilon$ are uniformly comparable. For mixing, put fixed positive-time
smoothing factors on either side of a middle convolution. The latter has
zero-mean $L^2$ norm at most $e^{-\gamma t}$. The outer factors have
uniform $L^2$ norms by smoothing, so their convolution kernel approaches
one uniformly as the middle physical time grows.
\end{proof}

Set $B_{\epsilon,L}=T_{\epsilon,L}^{m_\epsilon}$. For real $u$,
if $\lambda_{0,L}$ and $b_{0,L}$ are the respective top eigenvalues,
\begin{equation}
 -s_\epsilon^{-1}\log(B_{\epsilon,L}/b_{0,L})
 =-\tau^{-1}\log(T_{\epsilon,L}/\lambda_{0,L}).
 \label{eq:wdt-power-preserves-energy}
\end{equation}
Thus blocking changes neither energies nor spectral projections. It is not
replacement by $K_\epsilon-uV$ or by a heat-kernel action.

\subsubsection{Exact free-bridge expansion and a uniform convergence test}
Conditional on the coarse endpoint link configurations, free paths on
separate links are independent. Write $v_p=\chi_p+\bar\chi_p$,
$\|v_p\|_\infty\le J=2N$, and
\[
 F_p=\exp\!\left[u\tau\left(\tfrac12 v_p(U_0)
     +\sum_{j=1}^{m_\epsilon-1}v_p(U_j)+\tfrac12v_p(U_{m_\epsilon})\right)\right]-1.
\]
For a finite set $X$ of distinct plaquettes define
$M_{\epsilon,X}=\mathbb E_{\rm bridge}\prod_{p\in X}F_p$.
For complex $u$,
\[
 \|M_{\epsilon,X}\|_\infty\le b(u)^{|X|},\qquad
 b(u)=e^{J(s_0+\tau_0)|u|}-1.
\]
Independent link bridges factor this activity over shared-link connected
components. Let $P_\ell=p_\epsilon^{*m_\epsilon}(U'_\ell U_\ell^{-1})$.
The exact block kernel is
\[
 B(U',U)=\prod_\ell P_\ell\!
   \sum_{\{X_i\}\ {\rm link\!\!\!-disjoint}}\prod_iM_{\epsilon,X_i}.
\]
Absorb $P_\ell$ on the links of each occupied $X_i$ into its activity;
its norm is at most $[(1+\delta)^4b(u)]^{|X_i|}$. On the unoccupied links
write $P_\ell=1+q_\ell$, $\|q_\ell\|_\infty\le\delta$.
The same-slab exclusion of overlapping magnetic and temporal atoms is
retained in this identity.

\begin{proposition}[Temporal-mesh-independent scalar polymer criterion]
\label{prop:wdt-polymer-criterion}
Use coarse sites $(\ell,j)$. Temporal atoms have support size two;
a connected $r$-plaquette magnetic atom has support size at most $8r$.
Fix $\xi>0$ and suppose
\[
 \delta\le e^{-(4+2\xi)}/16,\qquad
 y=e^{16+8\xi}(1+\delta)^4b(u)\le1/256.
\]
Then the scalar hard-core polymer expansion of $\operatorname{Tr}B^M$
converges uniformly in spatial volume and temporal mesh, with a positive
exponential weight in coarse space-time support. In particular it converges
on a nonzero common disc, for example strictly inside
\[
 u_c=\frac1{J(s_0+\tau_0)}
 \log\!\left(1+\frac{e^{-(16+8\xi)}}{256(1+\delta)^4}\right).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A link lies in four spatial plaquettes. The plaquette shared-link graph
has degree twelve, and at most $144^{r-1}$ connected $r$-sets contain a
specified plaquette, by a depth-first spanning-tree encoding.
Thus at most $8\,144^{r-1}$ magnetic atoms of size $r$ contain a given
coarse site. If $b_A$ bounds an atom of support $S_A$, then
\[
 \sup_z\sum_{A\ni z}b_Ae^{(2+\xi)|S_A|}
 \le 2e^{4+2\xi}\delta+\frac{8y}{1-144y}
 \le\frac18+\frac1{14}=\frac{11}{56}<\frac14.
\]
Expand the scalar path integral and group admissible atoms into connected
components under support intersection. A component $\Gamma$ has weight
$w(\Gamma)=\int\prod_{A\in\Gamma}f_A\,d\mu$ and
$|w(\Gamma)|\le\prod_A b_A$. Disjoint components factor. Same-slab
exclusions only remove terms in the positive majorant.

For completeness the connected-component majorant follows from
$T_A^{(0)}=b_Ae^{(1+\xi)|S_A|}$ and
\[
 T_A^{(n+1)}=b_Ae^{(1+\xi)|S_A|}
       \exp\!\left(\sum_{B:S_B\cap S_A\ne\varnothing}T_B^{(n)}\right).
\]
Rooted trees overcount connected atom sets. Induction bounds this iteration
by $b_Ae^{(2+\xi)|S_A|}$. Consequently
\[
 \sup_z\sum_{\Gamma\ni z}|w(\Gamma)|
 e^{|\operatorname{supp}\Gamma|+\xi\sum_{A\in\Gamma}|S_A|}
 \le11/56.
\]
Summing over the sites of an incompatible polymer is the
Kotecky--Preiss criterion. Apply the abstract cluster and marked-correlation
theorems of Ueltschi (arXiv:math-ph/0304003v3, Theorems 1--3).
This invocation is of a scalar polymer theorem after an exact path
representation, not a theorem about a noncommutative transfer logarithm.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform vacuum and full-source construction]
\label{thm:wdt-vacuum}
In a smaller common disc, bounded local multiplication-observable
correlations have thermodynamic and zero-temperature limits, are analytic
in $u$, and satisfy
\[
 |\langle AB\rangle_c|\le C_{A,B}e^{-\xi d(S_A,S_B)}.
\]
For real $u$ the finite-volume state is the actual Perron vacuum of the
Wilson transfer. Its excitation gap has a uniform lower bound
$\xi/(s_0+\tau_0)$ in electric-energy units.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Introduce marks $e^{\theta A}-1$ and differentiate the convergent log
partition function. Its connected terms join the marks, and the support
weight bounds their separation. Boundary-sensitive connected terms have
exponentially small tails, giving the limits and their interchange.
In finite volume the positive compact transfer has a simple positive
Perron eigenvector, which is the zero-temperature trace limit. A centered
source has a positive moment measure for $D_\epsilon=B/b_0$. Its moments
are at most $C_Ae^{-\xi j}$, so its spectral support is below $e^{-\xi}$.
Bounded multiplication functions times the strictly positive vacuum are
dense, proving the claimed gap. This argument is at fixed spatial spacing.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{All-orders matching of full source correlations}
The same scalar construction applies to the Hamiltonian block kernel
$e^{-s(H_E-uV)}$. The previous minorization also proves
$p_\epsilon^{*m}\to h_s$ uniformly on the compact group when
$m\tau\to s>0$: discard the exponentially unlikely binomial part and
use a fixed positive-time heat factor to dominate all representation tails.
On a fixed finite link support, expand the bounded potential by insertion
number $n$. At least one of its $n+1$ free gaps has duration at least
$s/(n+1)$. Smoothing on this gap gives the summable bound
\[
 C_Y[1+((n+1)/s)^{D|\operatorname{links}(Y)|/2}]
 (s\|V_Y\||u|)^n/n!.
\]
For fixed $n$, omit short free gaps, apply kernel and Riemann-sum
convergence, and then restore the omitted gaps. The displayed bound permits
summation over $n$. Thus each finite block activity converges uniformly,
including endpoints and complex $u$. The common polymer majorant permits
its substitution into the infinite marked series.

For the normalized odd Wilson sources, let $C_{\epsilon,j}^W(x;u)$
be the complete, \emph{unprojected}, vacuum source-correlation matrix at
time $j s_\epsilon$, and let $C_j^H(x;s_\epsilon,u)$ be its Hamiltonian
comparator. For some common $\mu,\nu>0$,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{j\ge0,x}e^{\mu|x|_1+\nu j}
 \max_{\alpha,\beta}|(C_{\epsilon,j}^W-C_j^H)_{\alpha\beta}(x)|\to0.
 \label{eq:wdt-full-correlation-matching}
\end{equation}
No uniform quadratic temporal rate is asserted. Polynomial approximation
of continuous functions of the positive block transfer also gives source
spectral-measure convergence and convergence at every fixed positive
physical time. If the Hamiltonian G18 shell and its external gaps are taken
as additional premises, source Gram matrices in a common Borel energy
window converge in unweighted matrix norm. This last observation does not
establish an exponentially local Wilson Riesz projection.

\begin{theorem}[Source-visible plaquette window without assuming a pole]
\label{thm:wdt-visible-window}
For SU(3), fix $E_s=8/3$ and $0<\Delta<E_s$. There is a further common
small real coupling interval in which the source Gram matrix in the energy
window $I_\Delta=(E_s-\Delta,E_s+\Delta)$ obeys
\[
 J_\epsilon(k,u)^*1_{I_\Delta}(G_\epsilon)J_\epsilon(k,u)
 \succeq \tfrac12 I_3.
\]
This is uniform on finite-volume momentum grids and holds in the limiting
energy--momentum representation almost everywhere. The projection is Borel,
not asserted to be a Riesz projection.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $c=e^{-s_\epsilon E_s}$ and use the first three full source moments:
\[
 \mathcal V=C_2-2cC_1+c^2C_0\succeq0.
\]
At $u=0$, $(C_0,C_1,C_2)=(I,cI,c^2I)$ exactly. Positivity for both signs
of real $u$ gives $\mathcal V'(0)=0$. Uniform analyticity hence gives
$\|\mathcal V\|_{\mu,\sharp}\le Ku^2$ and $C_0\succeq3I/4$
in a smaller common interval. For
\[
 d_*=e^{-(s_0+\tau_0)E_s}(1-e^{-s_0\Delta})>0
\]
functional calculus gives
$1_{I_\Delta^c}(G_\epsilon)\le d_*^{-2}(D_\epsilon-c)^2$.
The window Gram is therefore at least $C_0-d_*^{-2}\mathcal V$, which
is at least $I/2$ if $Ku^2\le d_*^2/4$.
In infinite volume the window is defined by the corresponding positive
Borel interval of $D_\epsilon$; no logarithm on a possible kernel of that
limiting transfer is assumed.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
A bounded-below three-source synthesis into a window is not totality of
that window and need not have invariant range. The remaining task is the
vacuum-dressed excited-transfer contour estimate, or an irreducible tube
construction reaching the plaquette spectral neighborhood. These results
neither select an isolated internal carrier nor establish the spatial
continuum limit. The positive measure
$(1-\eta^2)\delta_{E_s}+\eta^2(2\Delta)^{-1}1_{[E_s-\Delta,E_s+\Delta]}dE$
has uniformly converging, exponentially decaying correlators but no
isolated spectral point at $E_s$ when $\eta>0$; it is a negative control
for confusing the proved correlation matching with excited-band isolation.
