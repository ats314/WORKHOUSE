\subsection{Uniform calibrated Wilson kinetic window}
\label{sec:wilson-kinetic-window}
Fix $N\ge3$ and normalized Haar measure on $SU(N)$. Use
$\operatorname{tr}_F(T_aT_b)=\delta_{ab}/2$ and put
\[
 p_\epsilon(U)=Z_\epsilon^{-1}e^{-\phi(U)/\epsilon},\qquad
 \phi(U)=2(N-\operatorname{ReTr}U),\qquad
 \lambda_R(\epsilon)=\frac1{d_R}\int p_\epsilon(U)\overline{\chi_R(U)}\,dU.
\]
All $\lambda_R$ are positive: the exponential has a character expansion with
nonnegative tensor-product multiplicities, and every irrep appears. They are
at most one because $p_\epsilon$ is a probability density. Define
\[
 \tau_F(\epsilon)=-\frac2{C_F}\log\lambda_F(\epsilon),\qquad
 k_\epsilon(R)=-\frac{\log\lambda_R(\epsilon)}{\tau_F(\epsilon)},\qquad
 K_{\epsilon,L}=\sum_\ell k_\epsilon(\ell).
\]
The calibration gives $k_\epsilon(F)=k_\epsilon(\bar F)=C_F/2$ exactly. The
fixed-irrep Laplace expansion gives $\tau_F=\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2)$ and
$k_\epsilon(R)=C_R/2+O_R(\epsilon^2)$, but is not uniform over all $R$.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform exclusion of high representations]
\label{lem:wilson-no-pollution}
For each fixed $N$ there are $c_N,\epsilon_1>0$ such that
\[
 k_\epsilon(R)\ge c_N\min\{C_R,\epsilon^{-1}\}
 \quad(0<\epsilon<\epsilon_1)
\]
for every irrep $R$. In particular every fixed low-energy window contains a
common finite set of link representations as $\epsilon\downarrow0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $r(U)$ be distance from identity for the metric with $C_2=-\Delta$, and
let $h_s$ be the density of $e^{-sC_2/2}$. Along a shortest geodesic
$U=e^{iX}$ one has $r(U)^2=2\operatorname{tr}X^2$ and
$\phi(U)\le r(U)^2/2$. The unique nondegenerate minimum of $\phi$ and
compactness give $Z_\epsilon\le B_N\epsilon^{(N^2-1)/2}$.
The Gaussian upper heat-kernel bound, specialized to the static bi-invariant
metric, gives
\[
 h_s(U)\le A_Ns^{-(N^2-1)/2}e^{-r(U)^2/(4s)}.
\]
One precise external input is Buzano--Yudowitz, arXiv:2007.07112,
Theorem 1.5 and Corollary 1.4, with their heat time replaced by $s/2$.
Taking $s=\epsilon/4$ proves $p_\epsilon\ge a_Nh_{\epsilon/4}$ for some
$a_N>0$. This comparison is used on the nonnegative Dirichlet integrand:
\[
 \langle f,(1-C_p)f\rangle
 =\frac12\int p(U)\|L_Uf-f\|^2\,dU.
\]
Consequently $1-\lambda_R\ge a_N(1-e^{-\epsilon C_R/8})$. Now use
$-\log x\ge1-x$, $\tau_F\le2\epsilon$ and
$1-e^{-x}\ge(1-e^{-1})\min\{x,1\}$. For example
$c_N=a_N(1-e^{-1})/16$ suffices. If $k_\epsilon(R)\le E$ and
$\epsilon<c_N/E$, then $C_R\le E/c_N$; there are finitely many such
highest weights.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Calibrated Wilson free physical shell]
\label{thm:wilson-physical-window}
For every fixed $N\ge3$, there is $\epsilon_0(N)>0$ such that for all
$0<\epsilon<\epsilon_0(N)$ and periodic $L^3$ lattices with $L\ge3$,
\[
 \operatorname{spec}(K_{\epsilon,L}|_{\mathcal H_{\rm phys}^{(0)}})
 \cap[0,5C_F/2)=\{0,2C_F\}.
\]
The vacuum is unique and the $2C_F$ eigenspace is exactly the oriented
fundamental-plaquette span. Its neutral charge-odd part has dimension $3L^3$.
The external free physical margin is at least $C_F/2$, uniformly in volume.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma turns fixed-irrep convergence into uniform convergence
on every bounded-energy set. Shrinking $\epsilon_0$ gives
\[
 k_\epsilon(R)\ge C_F/2\ (R\ne1),\qquad
 k_\epsilon(R)>5C_F/8\ (R\notin\{1,F,\bar F\}),
\]
and $k_\epsilon(R)>5C_F/6$ for every nonzero zero-$N$-ality irrep.
The strict inequalities follow from the Casimir shelves in the electric
window theorem; the last uses $C_R\ge N$. An energy below $5C_F/2$ has
at most four nontrivial links. Gauge invariance forbids degree-one support.
The support classification is therefore the same as for the Casimir
Hamiltonian: a single three- or four-cycle. The $L=3$ winding triangle and
$L=4$ winding four-cycle require zero $N$-ality and violate the new shelves.
The remaining four-cycle is an elementary plaquette; degree-two intertwiners
transport the same irrep around it, and the shelf excludes everything but
$F,\bar F$. Calibration fixes its energy to $2C_F$. Orthogonality gives the
stated dimension and the upper window gives the margin.
\end{proof}
For $SU(3)$ the window is $[0,10/3)$ with levels $0,8/3$ and margin $2/3$.
The temporal threshold remains existential. On the kinematic space, after
shrinking it once more, the circle $|z-8/3|=1/12$ has clearance at least
$1/24$, uniformly in volume. This uses the nearest additive Casimir values
$14/6,16/6,17/6$ and the common finite-label reduction, not a physical-sector
identification before imposing the constraints.

\subsection{Actual finite-step shell and source coefficients}
The retuned Wilson transfer is
\[
 T_{\epsilon,L}(u)=e^{\tau_FuV_L/2}e^{-\tau_FK_{\epsilon,L}}
                         e^{\tau_FuV_L/2},\qquad
 G_{\epsilon,L}(u)=-\tau_F^{-1}\log T_{\epsilon,L}(u).
\]
Its spatial Wilson coefficient is $\beta_s=2Nu\tau_F$, with
$\beta_t=2N/\epsilon$. In general $G_{\epsilon,L}(u)\ne K_{\epsilon,L}-uV_L$.
For a degenerate free shell $P$ of energy $E_s$ with scalar $PVP$, direct
second-order transfer perturbation and its folded term give
\[
 [u^2]G_{\epsilon,\mathrm{eff}}
 =-\sum_{m\notin P}d_\tau(E_m-E_s)PVQ_mVP,\qquad
 d_\tau(\Delta)=\frac\tau2\coth\frac{\tau\Delta}{2}.
\]
Indeed, with $c=e^{-\tau E_s}$ and $d=e^{-\tau E_m}$, their sum is
$\tau^2(c+d)/4+\tau^2(c+d)^2/[4(c-d)]
=\tau^2c(c+d)/[2(c-d)]$. Applying $-\log/\tau$ and canceling the internal
$(PVP)^2$ term proves the formula.
The original complete channel and centre-charge classification then gives
\[
 w_\rho^W=-\frac{d_\rho}{N^2}d_\tau(C_F+k_\epsilon(\rho)),\quad
 A_W=w_1^W+w_{\rm Adj}^W,\quad
 B_W=w_{\Lambda^2F}^W+w_{\operatorname{Sym}^2F}^W,\quad t_W=B_W-A_W.
\]
For $SU(3)$ set $E_F=8/3$ and
\[
 \ell_W=A_W+B_W+2d_\tau(E_F),\qquad
 \sigma_W=-d_\tau(4k_\epsilon(6)-E_F),\qquad
 s_{2,W}=\sigma_W+2d_\tau(E_F)+12\ell_W-4t_W.
\]
Vacuum subtraction cancels the volume-dependent link-disjoint bubbles, so
\[
 T_{\le2}h_{\epsilon,u}^W(k)
 =\left(\frac83+u+s_{2,W}u^2\right)I+t_Wu^2B(k)B(k)^\dagger.
\]
Exact SU(3) Gaussian moments give
\[
 \tau_F=\epsilon+\frac7{24}\epsilon^2+\frac{13}{144}\epsilon^3+O(\epsilon^4),
 \quad k_\epsilon(8)=\frac32-\frac5{96}\epsilon^2+O(\epsilon^3),
 \quad k_\epsilon(6)=\frac53-\frac5{72}\epsilon^2+O(\epsilon^3).
\]
Consequently
\[
 t_W=\frac5{612}+\frac{175}{280908}\epsilon^2+O(\epsilon^3),\qquad
 s_{2,W}=\frac{11}{306}-\frac{89159}{2247264}\epsilon^2+O(\epsilon^3).
\]
For the normalized literal source $O_p^-=(\chi_p-\bar\chi_p)/\sqrt2$, the
complete-shell projected Gram kernel satisfies
\[
 G_\epsilon^W(k,u)=I-2u\,d_{\tau_F}(8/3)I+O(u^2),\qquad
 [u](G_\epsilon^W-G^H)=-\frac49\epsilon^2I+O(\epsilon^3).
\]
This is an absolute projected Gram statement, not a normalized spectral fraction.

At every fixed magnetic order, only a finite set of tensor-power irreps and
linked supports can occur. The calibrated finite-space kinetic error and
symmetric product logarithm therefore give $O(\epsilon^2)$ matching of that
order's energy and source kernels in the spatially weighted convolution norm,
with constants independent of sufficiently large spatial volume. Summing those
orders still requires a uniform discrete-time marked-cluster majorant and
identification of its sums with the actual vacuum and complete Riesz shell.
Neither the free kinetic theorem nor the finite-order identities above alone
establish that summed Wilson-band/source result.
