\section{Lineage and retained sources}
\label{sec:lineage}

This document has two predecessors, both preserved, and it supersedes
neither in the sense of replacing them: it consolidates them.

\subsection{The reference editions}

A sequence of reference editions carries the mathematical core:
\emph{Exact Gauge-Spectral Structure and Controlled Wilson Transport},
reaching a fourth edition on 12 September 2026 and a fifth on the same
day, the fifth adding an agent guide, a source atlas and source
records around the retained fourth-edition core.

Those editions supply material this document did not have, and it is
imported here rather than paraphrased: the exceptional
\(\SU(3)\) Haar projectors and the reduction of physical clusters, the
energy-preserving private-path reduction, the isotropic pentagonal cap
band as a second physical geometry, flat winding holonomy and uniformly
observed spatial modes, the one-step physical comparison mechanisms
(Schur excess about a nonreducing reference, thermodynamic response,
all-order temporal matching), and the exact sixth-order comparison at
\(q = 3\). Their curated per-source judgement is the basis of
Appendix~\ref{app:atlas}, and their statement-to-evidence mapping is
the basis of Appendix~\ref{app:crosswalk}.

\subsection{The master edition}

The other predecessor is the master edition of this program, which
supplies the structure of Parts~\ref{part:exact}--\ref{part:continuum},
the generated apparatus, and the material the reference editions do not
contain at all: the six obstructions of \S\ref{sec:breaks}, the
refutation register of \S\ref{sec:register}, and the dead routes of
\S\ref{sec:obligations}.

That last omission is worth naming precisely, because it is the reason
the two documents needed consolidating rather than merely
cross-referencing. The reference editions contain no adversarial
analysis: no obstruction, no dead route, no \texttt{cannot\_decide}
annotation, no retraction. An agent working from them alone would
re-run instruments the register already shows cannot decide the
question they are aimed at, and would re-derive mechanisms this program
has withdrawn. Both failures have happened here before, and the second
half of \S\ref{sec:obligations} exists to stop them happening again.

\subsection{What was carried, and how}

Curated judgement --- what is located in a source, and the scope within
which it may be used --- cannot be derived from a ledger, so it is
carried as data and kept under review. Everything checkable about a
source is re-derived when this document is built.

The distinction has already earned itself. Building
Appendix~\ref{app:atlas} against the current tree resolves
\AtlasVerified{} of \AtlasRecords{} source records byte-for-byte;
the remainder split into three states that a transcribed atlas would
have reported identically and wrongly. Four are maintained ledgers and
generated catalogues whose bytes have moved on, which is expected and
means only that the note should be re-read. Thirteen are real sources
in the wider archive or in sibling worktrees, outside the repository
boundary and unhashable from here. One is a repository path that
\texttt{main} does not yet carry, because its source is still on an
open pull request --- the curation being ahead of the branch rather
than wrong.

None of those four states is an error, and none of them is the same as
the others. A hand-maintained atlas states a digest once and goes on
stating it.

\subsection{Attribution}

The reference editions and the master edition were produced with
substantial assistance from different automated systems, working from
the same repository and ledgers. Where they disagreed, the ledger
decided; where the ledger was silent, the disagreement is recorded
rather than resolved by preference.

This matters more than a conventional acknowledgement would, because
the program has a measured result about exactly this. Thirteen
independent agent summaries of this corpus were audited against their
primary sources and all thirteen overstated in at least one respect
(Appendix~\ref{app:provenance}). Material imported here from a
companion edition was therefore verified against the ledgers before
import, in the same way and for the same reason, and the corrections
are named where they land.
