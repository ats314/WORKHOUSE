## 3. Complete carrier-visible implication chain

### Step A: reference band

Choose a fixed physical coarse lattice spacing/time scale `A` and a reference small-`u` transfer operator `T_*` in the proved fixed-spacing CMP domain. In each momentum fiber let `P_*(k)` be the complete carrier Riesz projection and let `W_*(k):C^3->K_*(k)` be the **unprojected** literal Wilson source synthesis map. The fixed-spacing chain gives, fiberwise,

`W_*(k)^* P_*(k) W_*(k) >= z_* I_3`,

an external transfer gap `d_*>0`, and the separately recorded uniform unprojected-source norm `sup_k||W_*(k)||<=M_*`. The latter is distinct from the projected frame bound and is obtained from the exponentially summable unprojected Wilson-source Gram kernel; it must not be inferred from boundedness of `P_*W_*` alone.

### Step B: exact blocked Wilson pair

Choose a scaling subsequence with `b_n=A/a_n` a positive integer and align the block and reflection planes. For every fine cutoff theory, apply the aligned gauge-covariant path-product block map to the fixed physical scale `A`. Do not project the induced measure back to a one-coupling Wilson ansatz; retain the full pushforward OS system. Exact blocking can generate temporally nonlocal interactions, so no finite-range one-slab kernel is presumed. The deterministic map gives:

- exact pushforward for that fine theory by definition;
- reflection and positive-time compatibility;
- exact block-time covariance;
- an exact fine pullback of the coarse extended Wilson-loop source.

Assuming the fine and coarse transfers are positive and injective, OS reconstruction then provides an isometry `J_n` and exact spectral-measure preservation inside its range.

### Step C: transfer and source entry

First construct a specified gauge-, `C`-, cubic-, and coarse-translation-covariant unitary `U_n` identifying the induced coarse OS Hilbert/source representation with the reference. This common representation is itself a model-specific theorem obligation and logically precedes the following inequalities. After alias completion as in Step D, prove fiberwise

`||(T_hat_c,n(k)-T_*(k))(z-T_*(k))^(-1)|| <= q<1`

uniformly on the reference isolating contour and on physical momenta near zero. This is the contour-relative form of TE.

Also prove

`||W_hat_c,n(k)-W_*(k)|| <= epsilon_W`.

If `eta_P` is the resulting Riesz-projection error and

`z_ent=z_*-2M_*epsilon_W-epsilon_W^2-M_*^2 eta_P>0`,

then

`W_c,n(k)^* P_c,n(k) W_c,n(k) >= z_ent I_3`.

For the separated-time normalization used in the matrix KL theorem, fix `tau_0>0` and, at the completed `p=0` fiber, define

`G_n(tau_0)=W_c,n(0)^* exp(-2tau_0 H_c,n(0)) W_c,n(0)`

and

`Z_n(tau_0)=G_n(tau_0)^(-1/2) W_c,n(0)^*P_c,n(0)exp(-2tau_0H_c,n(0))P_c,n(0)W_c,n(0)G_n(tau_0)^(-1/2)`.

Let the transported island lie below a uniform physical energy `E_+`. Its filtered numerator obeys

`W_c,n(0)^* P_c,n(0) exp(-2tau_0 H_c,n(0)) P_c,n(0) W_c,n(0) >= exp(-2tau_0 E_+) z_ent I_3`.

The filtered Gram matrix is at most `(M_*+epsilon_W)^2 I`, and it is invertible because it dominates this positive carrier numerator. Therefore

`lambda_min Z_n(tau_0) >= exp(-2tau_0 E_+) z_ent/(M_*+epsilon_W)^2>0`.

This is a cutoff-level lower bound for the normalized **whole-island** weight, not yet continuum H3. After the uniform thermodynamic/alias/fiber passage of Step D, it supplies H3 if the completed `p=0` fiber is exactly one irreducible cubic `T1` copy of rank three and the transfer commutes with the cubic action. Schur's lemma then makes the transfer scalar on that fiber, giving a genuine triply degenerate atom at some `M_n`. If completion creates several `T1` copies, this conclusion no longer follows from Schur alone; one must instead prove a uniformly weighted sub-island whose physical-energy diameter tends to zero.

> No audited unpublished file proves uniform TE and SE for the exact coarse transfer/source pair generated by blocking the four-dimensional SU(3) Wilson scaling sequence, together with translation/alias completion that retains one controlled rank-three rest atom (or a shrinking substitute).

4. The SU(3) local `p_3` calculation proves a nonzero block source anchor, but no uniform polymer/transfer remainder estimate promotes it to SE.
5. The attempted Wilson block-transfer theorem fails its repeated-index moment step and cannot beat its volume-exponential error with logarithmic blocks; it is also not an OS strip-transfer comparison.
6. Exact blocking generates higher-character and multi-loop terms. No file bounds the full induced coarse pair in the contour/source norms above.

Thus the proof has not stopped because accepted history says it must. The abstract expressions are fully defined once `U_n` and the completed `p=0` representation are constructed; those constructions are themselves presently unproved model-specific obligations, followed by the displayed TE/SE inequalities.
