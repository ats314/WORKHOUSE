This folder contains a curated subset of **derivations, mechanisms, and computations** from the project files that appear *most novel* or *most likely to develop into publishable theory*.

These notes are **not** the full project. They are a cleaned “greatest-hits” extraction emphasizing:

- the **Haar-geometry → curvature → spectral-gap** mechanism,
- the **operator-Riccati / PBH flow** comparison principle idea,
- **UV polylog control + IR topology decoupling** as a route to *local* uniformity,
- the **Mosco-stability engine** for lifting functional inequalities to the continuum,
- and the strongest **numerical/computational evidence** present in the provided files.

## Contents

1. `SELECTED_01_Haar_Curvature_Wilson_Mass.md`  
   Local lattice theorem: in the pulled-back Haar metric, the Bakry–Émery tensor of the Wilson measure is **strictly positive on horizontal directions** in a small-angle sector, giving a geometric “mass-from-curvature” term.

2. `SELECTED_02_Operator_Riccati_PBH_Flow.md`  
   The “PBH flow” viewpoint: an **operator Riccati inequality** for the evolving physical Hessian, and how a positive source term forces a protected eigenvalue floor (the mass gap mechanism).

3. `SELECTED_03_UV_LogForest_IR_Decoupling.md`  
   A (conditionally rigorous) package of ideas: **polylog UV control** and **local-vs-topological decoupling**, designed to explain why *local observables* can have a uniform gap even when global topological modes are slow.

4. `SELECTED_04_Mosco_Curvature_Stability.md`  
   The analytic engine: how **Mosco convergence of Dirichlet forms** can preserve the curvature-dimension inequality (and hence the spectral gap) into the continuum.

5. `SELECTED_05_Dichotomy_and_OS_Reconstruction.md`  
   A conceptual reduction (dichotomy theorem) + the standard OS bridge from **Euclidean spectral data** to a **Hamiltonian** with a mass gap.

6. `SELECTED_06_Evidence_Curvature_Mass_Fit.md`  
   The strongest concrete evidence file: a linear fit relating a curvature-derived scale to known lattice mass-gap values, with reproducible code + plot.

7. `SELECTED_07_Computation_HeatKernel_SuperExtractor.md`  
   A computational sanity-check: scanning a “super-extractor” Hessian bound vs flow time (SU(2) model), with the exact script output embedded.
