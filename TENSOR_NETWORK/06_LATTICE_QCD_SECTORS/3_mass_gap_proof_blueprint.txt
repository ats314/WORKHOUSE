Title: Blueprint for a Mass-Gap Proof in SU(3) Yang–Mills (From Chat Synthesis)

This document summarizes the *structural* blueprint we converged on for what a Clay-level
mass-gap proof would have to show, in operator/transfer-matrix language. It is not itself
a proof; it is a roadmap.


1. Lattice Construction and Transfer Matrix

Start from the Wilson lattice gauge theory with action S[U], for SU(3):

    S[U] = β Σ_p (1 - (1/3) ReTr(U_p)),

with β = 6/g^2.

From the Euclidean lattice action, use reflection positivity and Osterwalder–Seiler-type
constructions to define:

- a Hilbert space H(a) of states on a time-slice,
- a transfer matrix T(a) acting on H(a),
- a Hamiltonian H(a) via T(a) = e^{-a H(a)}.

Here a is the lattice spacing in the temporal direction.


2. Physical Hilbert Space via Gauss’s Law

Define the Gauss operators G_x^a at each site x (generators of local SU(3) gauge transformations).

The physical Hilbert space is:

    H_phys(a) = { |ψ⟩ ∈ H(a) : G_x^a |ψ⟩ = 0, ∀ x,a }.

The Hamiltonian relevant for the mass gap is H(a) restricted to H_phys(a).


3. Spectral Definition of the Mass Gap

Let E_n(a) be the eigenvalues of H(a) on H_phys(a):

    H(a) |ψ_n(a)⟩ = E_n(a) |ψ_n(a)⟩,  E_0(a) ≤ E_1(a) ≤ …

Define the mass gap at lattice spacing a as:

    m(a) = E_1(a) - E_0(a).

On the other hand, for the transfer matrix T(a):

    T(a) |ψ_n(a)⟩ = λ_n(a) |ψ_n(a)⟩,

with λ_n(a) = e^{-a E_n(a)} and λ_0(a) > λ_1(a) ≥ λ_2(a) ≥ …

Then:

    m(a) = - (1/a) log (λ_1(a) / λ_0(a)).


4. The Core Inequality Needed

For each fixed a (small enough), the key operator inequality one wants is a uniform
spectral-gap bound of the form:

    H(a) ≥ E_0(a) + m P_⊥,

on H_phys(a), where:

- P_⊥ is the orthogonal projection onto the complement of the vacuum |0⟩,
- m > 0 is a constant independent of a (for sufficiently small a).

Equivalently, in terms of T(a):

    λ_1(a) / λ_0(a) ≤ e^{-a m},

with the same m > 0 independent of a.


5. Continuum Limit

One must then:

- Construct a continuum Hilbert space H and Hamiltonian H (a → 0 limit).
- Show that the spectra E_n(a) converge appropriately to E_n (continuum).
- Show that the inequality passes to the limit:

    H ≥ E_0 + m P_⊥,

with m > 0.

This yields a strictly positive continuum mass gap:

    m_phys = E_1 - E_0 ≥ m > 0.


6. What Needs to Be Proved to Get the Inequality

The inequality H(a) ≥ E_0(a) + m P_⊥ can be approached by:

- Understanding H_E (electric term) on H_phys(a):
    H_E(a) = (g^2 / 2) Σ E^2.

  One would like to prove that, on H_phys(a), H_E(a) has no arbitrarily small eigenvalues
  other than the vacuum → a Poincaré-type inequality in the gauge-invariant sector.

- Controlling H_B (magnetic plaquette term) as a relatively bounded perturbation:

    |⟨ψ| H_B(a) |ψ⟩| ≤ c ⟨ψ| H_E(a) |ψ⟩ + C,

  for some c<1 and finite C, uniformly in a.

Combining these would give a coercivity bound for H(a) on H_phys(a).


7. Status

This blueprint matches the *shape* of what is widely believed to be needed. However:

- The actual construction of H(a) and H in 4D SU(3) with all properties rigorously proven
  is itself nontrivial and not fully completed in the continuum.

- The key spectral-gap inequality is not proven in 4D.

Thus this document reflects a plausible structure of a proof but is not itself progress
on the Clay problem.
