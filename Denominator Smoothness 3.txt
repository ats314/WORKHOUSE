Denominator Smoothness
A perturbative coefficient's denominator is physically determined. A rough one — a large prime where only small primes belong — is nearly a proof that the number is a float wearing exact clothing. This is what that claim survives as after the machine checks it.

Verdict
The invariant is real, and reproduced against the corpus. But it is not one bound — it is a rank-and-order-indexed ceiling, and it is a forensic screen, not a proof. "37-smooth" is a fitted constant that the corpus's own sixth-order coefficient already exceeds.

Its correct home is not physics but provenance: it catches the failure CLAUDE.md calls the single most dangerous bug — an exact-looking Rational that is really a reconstructed float — with one number and no tolerance, where the two existing guards each need a partner or a type mismatch.

The right form of the check turned out not to be smoothness at all but a typed, exponent-sensitive divisor certificate den | QBOUND, derived per construction. Two order-4 scopes are now derived end-to-end from the corpus's own resolvent primitives — §07.

01
The ladder is real, monotone, and taller than "37"
Within one homogeneous series — the native string tension σn and the published Kogut–Pearson–Shigemitsu tn — only the perturbative order changes. The largest prime in the reduced denominator climbs with it and never falls. Every factorization below was reproduced with sympy.factorint on workhouse.constants.

order r	series (SU3)	denominator Pmax	new primes at this order	status
0	σ₀, t₀	3	2, 3	derived
2	σ₂, t₂	17	5, 17	derived
3	σ₃, t₃	17	—	derived
4	σ₄, t₄	37	7, 11, 13, 19, 29, 31, 37	observed
5	σ₅, t₅	37	—	observed
6	t₆ (KPS)	61	23, 43, 47, 53, 59, 61	observed
Pmax(r) = largest prime factor of the reduced denominator. "37-smooth" is exactly Pmax(r ≤ 5); order 6 reaches 61. KPS t₆ is the second rough denominator in the registry — not a violation, the ladder continuing.
Among the program's 57 internal named rationals, exactly one carries a denominator prime above the ladder — and it is the one already named ARTIFACT.

02
Two sources — and only the first is derived
The primes do not come from one place, and separating them is the whole game.

derived  The structural floor, orders 0–3
The fixed, rank-independent SU(3) hopping denominator (n²−1)(2n²−1)(4n²−9) — the resolvent structure formalized in Basic.lean — evaluates at n=3 to 8·17·27 = 3672 = 2³·3³·17. Its largest prime, 17 = 2n²−1, is the ceiling through third order. This is a genuine polynomial derivation: the same polynomial holds for every rank, not a fit to three points.

observed  The energy-gap staircase, orders ≥ 4
Higher primes enter as an LCM over Kogut–Susskind resolvent products — electric energy gaps E(L)=L·CF/2, one factor per intermediate state. The fingerprint is unmistakable and is the LCM's signature:

coefficient	denominator factorization	reading
SIGMA_4	2⁷·3⁶·5²·7·11·13·17³·19·29·31·37	small primes high powers
KPS_T6	2¹⁸·3¹⁰·5⁶·7⁴·17⁵ · (23·43·47·53·59·61)	large primes to first power
The primitive Haar-resolvent channel (cellular.py) is ≤5-smooth on its own; the large primes arrive only with the full band-kernel sum.
But the ceiling value (37, then 61) is read off three transcribed coefficients, not predicted from an independent state count. That is the honest line between derived and fitted — and it is exactly the ADR-0005 hazard: a counting bound that looks clean until the next order.

The prime-gap puzzle — the sharpest open question. The present primes are almost every prime below the ceiling, with pointed exceptions: 23 is absent at orders 4–5 though the larger 29, 31, 37 are present, and first appears at order 6; 41 is absent even at order 6. So the reachable set is not a magnitude cutoff — it is a genuine reachability predicate: p appears at order r iff some order-r intermediate state has electric energy divisible by p. Predicting these gaps from the state enumeration is what would turn the screen into a theorem.

03
The ceiling is rank and order — the correction the notes missed
The corpus's own CERT_SUN_closed_surface_stage1 certificate writes the second-order denominator in closed form: ∝ 1/((N−1)(N+1)(2N−3)(2N+3)(2N²−1)). The largest prime is a value of 2N²−1, which grows like O(N²) — so a legitimate coefficient is rough the moment the rank is large enough, at second order:

rank N	order	denominator	Pmax	source
8	2	282321 = 3²·13·19·127	127	2N²−1 = 127
10	2	…·199	199	2N²−1 = 199
11	2	2289500 = 2²·5³·19·241	241	2N²−1 = 241
An order-only ceiling of 37 false-positives every one of these. (And 4N²−9 = 391 = 17·23 at N=10 — the same 23 that is "missing" at SU(3) until order 6 is simply a rank-polynomial factor at another rank.)
So the robust, parameter-free discriminant is not smoothness — it is denominator shape. A legitimate rough denominator is either an LCM (small primes to high powers, large primes to first power) or equals another coefficient's numerator (a ratio of coefficients). An artifact is a single generic large prime with no LCM structure, no rank-polynomial factor, and no numerator twin — decidable without knowing the order or the rank.

04
The one artifact, and why roughness sees it
The sole rough denominator among the program's own constants is contradiction C20, "RUN15 internal display artifact" — resolved, but instructive.

LINKED_VACUUM_4  (intended, exact)

−1474623 / 1675520

1675520 = 2⁸·5·7·11·17

smooth

LINKED_VACUUM_4_ARTIFACT  (RUN15 print)

−521965902 / 593076541

593076541 = one 9-digit prime

rough

The two agree to relative 3.4×10⁻¹⁵ — about 27 ulps, ~14 significant digits. That is well inside where a near-miss detector must trust its 1e-9 threshold and needs both values present. Roughness needs neither: continued-fraction reconstruction of a printed float produces a generic denominator, and a generic denominator is rough with probability ≈ 1. The exact value's is 2⁸·5·7·11·17; the reconstruction's is a lone prime.

05
Honest calibration — a two-sided-error screen
Dickman's ρ supplies the false-positive rate the corpus check otherwise lacks: for a 9-digit denominator to be 37-smooth by chance is a ρ(5.7) ≈ 7×10⁻⁵ event. So a rough denominator is nearly a proof of a float — but "nearly", with named leaks:

channel	behavior	why
CF reconstruction (the C20 class)	caught	generic denom, rough; miss rate ~7e-5
decimal print, den = 10ᵏ	missed	2ᵃ5ᵇ is trivially smooth → needs a power-of-10 check
ratio of two coefficients	false-positive	denom = another coefficient's numerator; exempt it
legitimate SU(N ≥ 8)	false-positive	rank-polynomial primes ~ O(N²); index by N
A flag is a suspicion requiring the isolate-and-quantify follow-up the corpus mandates — never an automatic rejection.
A new artifact class surfaced in the sweep. Of the 23 corpus-wide rough denominators with q > 10⁹, several are display truncations — e.g. SIGMA_5's numerator recorded over four different truncated copies of its denominator — a distinct failure from C20's continued-fraction reconstruction, living in DOC_FLUX_constants_index.

06
Where it sits — and a real pedigree
Non-negotiable #3 is guarded today by two checks that are each half-blind. Smoothness fills the exact gap between them.

near_miss_pairs is relational — needs two values adjacent and agreeing within 1e-9. It catches C20 only because the twin happens to be recorded.
The type guard is type-based — isinstance(value, float) only. It is structurally blind to Rational(521965902, 593076541), which is a Rational.
Smoothness is intrinsic — one value, no tolerance, no partner. It fires on an orphan reconstruction with no recorded twin. (Honest caveat: no such live orphan exists in the corpus today; the advantage is argued, shown only under counterfactual twin-deletion.)
Von Staudt–Clausen is the rigorous precedent of the right shape. The theorem fixes the denominator of the Bernoulli number B2n — itself a perturbative/generating-function coefficient — as a product of small primes with the largest growing with order. Verified here: B₁₆ = −3617/510, and 510 = 2·3·5·17 — literally the corpus's own low-order support. The corpus fingerprint is a hybrid: a von-Staudt-like large-prime skeleton plus an LCM small-prime part. What is novel is not that perturbative denominators are smooth — it is inverting that fact into a friability-quantified forensic for float artifacts.

07
The certificate — from smoothness to a typed divisor bound
Smoothness is a screen; the sound object it points at is a localization ring ℤ[S⁻¹] (closed under +,−,× but not inversion — inverting an energy gap turns a numerator prime into a denominator one) and, stronger, an exponent-sensitive divisor certificate den | QBOUND. Divisibility catches an impossible exponent like 2¹⁰⁰ that prime-support never sees, and uses no fitted threshold. Both are scope-typed: same rank and order, different construction → different bound.

The mechanism, read off the engine — not fitted. The string-tension engine computes each resolvent denominator as ((L0−A)N² − B·N + C − L0) / (4N) — a quadratic in the rank N. At N=3 the factor 2N²−1 = 17 falls straight out; so a construction of resolvent depth d carries 17ᵈ. That single fact fixes the two scopes.

order-4 scope	resolvents	17-power	denominator support	QBOUND
marked-cluster (m₄ mass)	2	17²	superset reaches {…,47}	2¹³·3⁶·5⁴·7²·11²·13·17²·19·23·29·31·37·47
band-kernel / string-tension (SOS pencil + σ)	3	17³	truncates at 37	2¹⁷·3⁸·5⁷·(7·11·13·17·19·29·31·37)³
Each QBOUND derived from the construction's primitives (Casimir gaps, Haar/Gram weights, resolvent depth), independent of the coefficient values.
Both scopes were checked against the corpus coefficients:

The exponent bound discriminates constructions: C_SHP_HISTORICAL's 17³ fails the marked-cluster 17² — a scope mismatch that prime-support alone waves through.
Why the string sector stops at 37
The string tension sums only connected (linked-cluster) diagrams, and the raw connected denominator already carries no 47 — so 47 is never produced, not produced-and-cancelled. The reason it isn't produced: 37 is the cheapest large prime — reachable by a 2-link, 4-box intermediate state (0,4)+(1,3) — while 23, 41, 43, 47 each need a 5- or 6-box irrep a connected four-plaquette diagram can't build. The marked-cluster superset reaches 47 only because it "keeps every irrep branch before cancellation" — the high-excitation branches the physical string tension excludes.

Why 41 in particular is missing. Every resolvent prime needs a state solving ΣC ≡ 16 (mod p) for the SU(3) Casimir C(p,q)=p²+pq+q²+3p+3q. 41's cheapest carrier is a 3-body state; 43 and 47 have 2-body carriers — so 41 is deferred to a higher order, not forbidden, entering the ladder at order 8. The governing number theory is the norm form of the Eisenstein integers ℤ[ζ₃] — the class-number-1 field of Heegner number 3, i.e. SU(3) itself. (Euler's famous x²+x+41 is a different Heegner story, number 163; same numeral, unrelated theorem.)

Derived vs still observed. The depth (2 vs 3) and the large-prime exponents (tight — 17², 17³ hit exactly) come from the engine's resolvent structure. The small-prime exponents are a Haar-plus-gap upper envelope (valid, loose). The one piece still observed: the exact 23/47 truncation — a clean box/link cap drops 29 and 31 that do appear, so the exact reachable set needs the walled-Brauer contraction module, whose input lives outside the repo.

08
The candidate, and the test that would settle it
Stated in the ledger's shape — a claim with a falsifier, because a candidate without one is an analogy:

# unifying_candidates (draft — provenance, not physics)
- id: U4
  claim: A primitive perturbative coefficient (not a ratio of two
    coefficients) has a reduced denominator whose largest prime factor is
    bounded by a monotone ceiling P_max(order, N), = the largest prime
    factor of the SU(N) rank polynomials LCM'd over resolvent depth. It
    carries the LCM fingerprint: {2,3,5,17} to high powers, large primes
    to first power. A Rational stored as exact that violates it, with no
    ratio-of-coefficients origin, is a float reconstruction.
  falsified_by: one primitive coefficient re-derived exactly from the
    stated SU(N) definitions whose denominator prime exceeds P_max at its
    order and rank and is neither a rank-polynomial factor nor another
    coefficient's numerator; OR a proven float artifact reconstructing
    to a smooth denominator within the ceiling.
  status: conjectured  # derived: r≤3, + both order-4 scopes (§07); open: general r≥4 ceiling & the truncation predicate
The decisive test — run, and its ground truth does not yet exist. Pre-registered prediction for order 7, locked from orders ≤6 alone: P_max(t₇) = 61, prime set identical to t₆ (all primes ≤ 61 except 41), since new large primes enter only at even orders (2→17, 4→37, 6→61; orders 3 and 5 added none) and the set is monotone. The competing reading — max resolvent factor ≈ order·N² + O(1) (2N²−1=17, 4N²+1=37, 6N²+7=61) — would instead permit 7N²+4 = 67. That gap is what the test resolves.

But there is nothing to check against. KPS 1981 published the Hamiltonian string tension to O(g⁻²⁴) = order x⁶ — t₆ is their last coefficient; no Hamiltonian t₇ exists, in the corpus or the literature. The order 12–14 series (Münster, Drouffe–Zuber) are Euclidean — a different quantity §12 forbids comparing, and verified here to be trivially ≤7-smooth with a non-monotone ceiling. So scoring the prediction requires a native order-7 computation — exactly the corpus's own open G7/G8 territory, one order further. The test is decisive but not yet runnable; it converts "extend the series one order" from bookkeeping into the adjudication of whether P_max is derived.

09
Corrections to the original notes
The notes were about 90% right against the machine. Three points needed sharpening — each strengthens the invariant rather than weakening it:

"One violation in the entire table" → precisely, unique among the program's 57 internal constants. A second rough denominator exists — KPS_T6, max prime 61 — but it is an external sixth-order transcription whose roughness is the ladder continuing.
"37 is the ceiling" → 37 is P_max(r ≤ 5). The ceiling is rank-and-order-indexed and reaches 61 by order 6; at high rank a legitimate coefficient is rough at second order.
The reconstruction mechanism is "print to ~15 digits, then continued-fraction reconstruct," not limit_denominator of the exact double (which recovers the smooth value). And 33554467/33554393 is a ratio of two primes near 2²⁵ (CRT moduli) — a rough denominator the screen correctly flags, not a false-negative.
Method. Every factorization, count, and rate above was reproduced this session with sympy over workhouse.constants and corpus_registry.combined_table(), read-only. Corpus-wide census: 247 rough of ~1446 denominators; gated to q>10⁹, 23 rough / 90 smooth. Claims that could not be machine-verified are marked observed or fitted, never derived.

No document is authority; only a machine check is. This page is a report on checks — not one of them.
