## Proof of closure

### Lemma 15.1 — Haar/Ricci contribution is group-independent

**Statement.**
Let (G) be a compact simple Lie group with a bi-invariant metric. Then
[
\mathrm{Ric}_G ;\ge; \kappa_G, g_G
]
for some (\kappa_G>0) depending only on (G).

**Proof.**
For any compact Lie group with a bi-invariant metric,
[
\mathrm{Ric}(X,X)= -\tfrac12 \sum_i \langle [X,e_i],[X,e_i]\rangle .
]
If (G) is simple, the adjoint representation has no flat directions, hence the quadratic form is strictly positive definite. ∎

**Use in the proof.**
Only this inequality is used; no rank-1 structure appears.

---

### Lemma 15.2 — Plaquette potential expansion is representation-theoretic

**Statement.**
Let (\rho:G\to U(n)) be a faithful unitary representation. Define
[
\vartheta(g)=1-\frac1n\Re\mathrm{Tr}(\rho(g)).
]
Then for all compact Lie groups (G),
[
\vartheta(\exp X)=\frac{1}{2n\lambda_\rho}|X|^2+O(|X|^3).
]

**Proof.**
This follows from Taylor expansion of (\rho(\exp X)) at the identity and faithfulness of (\rho). No special property of (\mathfrak{su}(2)) is used. ∎

---

### Lemma 15.3 — Vacuum Hessian = Maxwell operator holds for all (G)

**Statement.**
At the vacuum configuration,
[
\nabla^2 S_W(U^{(0)})=\frac{\beta}{n\lambda_\rho}, d_1^*d_1
]
as an operator on 1-cochains.

**Proof.**
The derivation uses only:

* linearization of plaquette holonomy,
* faithfulness of (\rho),
* lattice cochain calculus.

The Lie algebra enters only through the inner product induced by (\rho). ∎

---

### Lemma 15.4 — Horizontal/vertical splitting is categorical

**Statement.**
For any compact (G),
[
\text{vertical directions}=\mathrm{im}(d_0),\qquad
\text{horizontal directions}=\ker(d_0^*).
]

**Proof.**
This follows from gauge invariance alone and does not depend on dimension or rank of (G). ∎
