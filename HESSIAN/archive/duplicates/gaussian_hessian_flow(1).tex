\section{Gaussian Toy Model: Exact Hessian Flow and Gap Decay}

Consider the initial Gaussian action
\[
S_0(x) = \frac{1}{2} x^\top A_0 x, \quad A_0 > 0,
\]
with density $\rho_0(x) = e^{-S_0(x)}$.

Let $\rho_t$ satisfy the heat equation $\partial_t \rho_t = \Delta \rho_t$. Then
\[
\rho_t = G_t * \rho_0,
\]
where $G_t$ is the heat kernel. Convolution of Gaussians is Gaussian, so
\[
\rho_t(x) = \exp\!\Big(-\frac{1}{2} x^\top \Sigma_t^{-1} x\Big)\times \text{(normalization)},
\]
with covariance
\[
\Sigma_t = \Sigma_0 + 2t\, I,\quad \Sigma_0 := A_0^{-1}.
\]

Thus the effective action is
\[
S_t(x) = \frac{1}{2} x^\top H_t x + \text{const},
\quad H_t = \Sigma_t^{-1}.
\]

\subsection{Eigenvalue flow}

Diagonalize $A_0$ as $A_0 = Q^\top \Lambda_0 Q$ with
$\Lambda_0 = \mathrm{diag}(\lambda_1(0),\dots,\lambda_d(0))$.
Then
\[
\Sigma_0 = A_0^{-1} = Q^\top \Lambda_0^{-1} Q,
\quad
\Sigma_t = Q^\top(\Lambda_0^{-1} + 2tI) Q,
\]
and
\[
H_t = \Sigma_t^{-1}
= Q^\top (\Lambda_0^{-1} + 2tI)^{-1} Q.
\]

So each eigenvalue of $H_t$ is
\[
\lambda_i(t)
= \frac{1}{\lambda_i(0)^{-1} + 2t}
= \frac{\lambda_i(0)}{1 + 2t\,\lambda_i(0)}.
\]

Differentiating,
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\lambda_i(t)
= -2\,\lambda_i(t)^2.
\]

Hence in this toy model the Hessian eigenvalues solve the scalar Riccati ODE
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\lambda = -2\lambda^2,\quad \lambda(0)>0,
\]
with explicit solution $\lambda(t) = \lambda(0)/(1+2t\lambda(0))$.

The smallest eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min}(t)$ is positive for all $t\ge 0$ but decays to zero
as $t\to \infty$ like $1/(2t)$, illustrating that in flat space with pure heat-flow RG,
bare convexity alone does not produce a permanent mass gap.
