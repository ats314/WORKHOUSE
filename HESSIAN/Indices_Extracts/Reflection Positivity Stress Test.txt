## Goal (precise)

Numerically test Osterwalder–Schrader (OS) reflection positivity for SU(N) lattice gauge theory with Wilson action:

* Show ( \langle F^\Theta F\rangle_\beta \ge 0 ) for a separating time plane (t=0.5), for a **generating set** of cylindrical functionals (F) measurable w.r.t. (t\ge 1) links, under the **right‑invariant** Haar measure parametrization.
* Cross‑check by building the (compressed) transfer matrix (T) on one time step and verifying (T\succeq 0) on a truncated gauge‑invariant subspace.

## Tests (in increasing strength)

1. **OS quadratic forms (Monte Carlo)**

* Choose a spanning set ( {F_i}_{i=1}^m ) with support in (t\ge 1). Use:

  * single‑plaquette characters (\chi_R(\square_{t=1})),
  * short Wilson lines ending on the reflection plane,
  * small Wilson loops fully in (t\ge 1),
  * products of two disjoint objects (to check cross‑terms).
* Estimate the Gram matrix (G_{ij}=\langle F_i^\Theta F_j\rangle) by MC over right‑invariant proposals.
* **Acceptance criterion:** all eigenvalues (\lambda_k(G)\ge -\varepsilon) with (\varepsilon) at roundoff/MC error. Report min‑eig with CI.

2. **Half‑space conditional positivity**

* Condition on links on the reflection plane (fix a configuration (B) on (t=0) time‑like links), integrate only (t\ge 1) links:
  [
  G_{ij}(B)=\int \overline{F_i(\Theta U)}F_j(U), e^{-S_W(U)} d\mu_{t\ge 1},.
  ]
* Sample several (B) (drawn from equilibrium). Check (G(B)\succeq 0) for each.

3. **Compressed transfer matrix (T)**

* Build (T) via one‑slice path integral between (t=0) and (t=1), then project to a **finite** gauge‑invariant basis:

  * Basis = products of a few fundamental characters on spatial plaquettes at (t=0) (or spin‑network states with small total rep labels).
  * Compute matrix elements by integrating the time‑like links only (links straddling 0↔1).
* Check (T) Hermitian (within num tol) and (T\succeq 0) (min‑eig ≥ 0 within tol). Also check (T=K^\dagger K) factorization via Cholesky if possible.

## Implementation skeleton (clean; fill in your kernels)

* **Right‑invariant proposals**: heatbath/over‑relaxation or small‑angle Gaussian in (\mathfrak{su}(N)) mapped by (U \mapsto U \exp(X)).
* **Reflection map**: implement once; unit test on link orientations + plaquettes crossing the plane.
* **Observables (F_i)**: define as pure functions of links in (t\ge 1), return complex scalars (characters).
* **MC estimator**: accumulate (G_{ij}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_n \overline{F_i(\Theta U^{(n)})} F_j(U^{(n)})).
* **Error bars**: batch or autocorrelation‑aware jackknife; print min‑eig ± CI.
* **Transfer matrix block**:

  1. Enumerate a small basis ({\phi_a}) at (t=0) (gauge‑invariant).
  2. For each (a,b), estimate (T_{ab}=\langle \overline{\phi_a^\Theta},\phi_b\rangle) with only the straddling links active (others clamped as per definition).
  3. Symmetrize (T\leftarrow \frac{1}{2}(T+T^\dagger)) and eigendecompose.

## Pitfalls (watch these)

* **Orientation bookkeeping** at the plane: time‑like links must be inverted under reflection; cross‑plaquettes need careful ordering.
* **Gauge fixing**: do **not** fix gauge across the plane in the OS test; only use gauge‑invariant functionals or gauge‑average basis states.
* **Finite‑basis artifacts** for (T): your truncation must be invariant under reflection and spatial gauge transformations.
* **Right‑ vs left‑invariant**: physics is invariant; discrepancies indicate implementation bugs (common: wrong sign in exponent, mishandled dagger).
