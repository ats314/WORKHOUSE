# Local Off-Diagonal Hessian Decay Under Refinement (H3)

This file contains the provable version of H3 as drafted in the chat: off-diagonal Hessian mixing between a fixed local block and a far block vanishes under lattice refinement due to finite-range locality.

---

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Local Off--Diagonal Hessian Decay]
\label{lem:offdiag_decay}

Let $B_R \subset M = \mathbb{T}^4$ be a fixed physical ball.
For each lattice spacing $a>0$, let $H_a(A)$ be the gauge--fixed lattice 
Yang--Mills Hessian at configuration $A$, acting on the tangent space 
$T_A \mathcal{X}_a \cong \mathfrak{su}(N)^{E(\Lambda_a)}$.

Let $T^{\mathrm{loc}}_{A,a}$ denote the subspace generated by link variations 
supported entirely inside $B_R$, and let 
$T^{\mathrm{far}}_{A,a}$ be the subspace generated by variations on links 
whose graph distance from $B_R$ is at least $L_a \equiv \mathrm{dist}_a(B_R,\mathrm{supp}(T^{\mathrm{far}}_{A,a}))$.

Then there exist constants $C>0$ and $\alpha>0$ such that for all 
$X \in T^{\mathrm{loc}}_{A,a}$, $Y \in T^{\mathrm{far}}_{A,a}$ with 
$\|X\|=\|Y\|=1$,
\[
\bigl| \langle X , H_a(A) Y \rangle \bigr|
\;\le\;
C \, a^{\alpha}.
\]

Equivalently,
\[
\| \Pi_{\mathrm{loc}} H_a(A)\, \Pi_{\mathrm{far}} \|
\;\longrightarrow\; 0
\qquad\text{as } a\to 0.
\]

This decay is uniform in volume, gauge configuration $A$, and instanton sector.
\end{lemma}
```

```tex
\begin{proof}
The Hessian $H_a(A)$ is the second derivative of the gauge-fixed lattice 
Yang--Mills action
\[
S_a(A) = S^{W}_a(A) + S^{\mathrm{gf}}_a(A),
\]
where both terms are \emph{local}: each plaquette term in $S^{W}_a$ depends 
only on the four link variables forming the plaquette, and each term in 
$S^{\mathrm{gf}}_a$ depends only on the finitely many links entering and 
exiting a given vertex.

Thus
\[
H_a(A)
=
\sum_{p \in \Lambda_a} H_{a,p}(A)
\;+\;
\sum_{v \in \Lambda_a} H_{a,v}^{\mathrm{gf}}(A),
\]
where $H_{a,p}$ is supported on the four links of plaquette $p$, and 
$H_{a,v}^{\mathrm{gf}}$ is supported on the star of $v$.

Each $H_{a,p}$ and $H_{a,v}^{\mathrm{gf}}$ has operator norm bounded by a 
constant independent of $a$ due to smoothness of the action on the compact 
group $SU(N)$.

Let $X$ be supported entirely on links inside the fixed physical region
$B_R$, and $Y$ supported on links whose \emph{graph distance} from $B_R$ is 
at least $L_a$.
For any plaquette $p$, 
$H_{a,p}(A)$ contributes to $\langle X, H_a(A) Y\rangle$ only if both $X$ and 
$Y$ have nonzero components on links of $p$.

But by construction of $T^{\mathrm{loc}}_{A,a}$ and $T^{\mathrm{far}}_{A,a}$,
no plaquette intersects both $B_R$ and the support of $Y$.
Thus all plaquettes intersecting $\operatorname{supp}(X)$ lie within a 
fixed physical distance of order $O(1)$ of $B_R$.
As $a\to0$, the lattice graph distance from $B_R$ to $\operatorname{supp}(Y)$ 
satisfies 
\[
L_a \sim a^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{dist}_{\text{phys}}(B_R,\operatorname{supp}(Y)),
\]
which diverges like $a^{-1}$.

Therefore the number of plaquettes $p$ for which 
$\operatorname{supp}(H_{a,p})$ intersects both $\operatorname{supp}(X)$ and 
$\operatorname{supp}(Y)$ is zero for all sufficiently small $a$.
Hence
\[
\langle X, H_{a,p}(A) Y\rangle = 0
\]
for every plaquette $p$ and similarly for the gauge-fixing terms.

Therefore 
\[
\langle X, H_a(A) Y\rangle = 0
\quad\text{for all sufficiently fine lattices.}
\]

In particular,
\[
\|\Pi_{\mathrm{loc}} H_a(A)\Pi_{\mathrm{far}}\| 
\le C a^\alpha
\]
for any $\alpha>0$, since the left-hand side is eventually zero.

This estimate is uniform in $A$ by the compactness of $SU(N)$ and the local 
boundedness of second derivatives of the action.

This proves the claim.
\end{proof}
```
