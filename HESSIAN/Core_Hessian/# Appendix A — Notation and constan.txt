# Appendix A — Notation and constants

**File status:** Established (definitions + direct combinatorics; any conditional facts are explicitly flagged).

This appendix is a symbol table. It contains definitions and fixed conventions only.


## 0. Global convention for traces

Throughout the project we fix a faithful unitary representation \(\rho:G\to U(n)\).

**Convention (trace).**  
Whenever \(\mathrm{Tr}(\cdot)\) or \(\Re\mathrm{Tr}(\cdot)\) appears with a group element \(g\in G\), it means
\[
\mathrm{Tr}(g)\equiv \mathrm{Tr}(\rho(g)),\qquad \Re\mathrm{Tr}(g)\equiv \Re\mathrm{Tr}(\rho(g)).
\]
Equivalently, we identify \(g\in G\) with \(\rho(g)\in U(n)\) *only for the purpose of taking traces*.


## A. Lattice geometry (finite periodic hypercubic lattice)

### A.1 The lattice and its cell sets
- Let \(d\in\mathbb N\) be the dimension (default in this project: \(d=4\)).
- Let \(\Lambda\) be the finite periodic \(d\)-dimensional hypercubic lattice (a discrete torus).
- Vertex set: \(V(\Lambda)\).
- Oriented link set: \(E(\Lambda)\). (Each geometric edge appears once with a chosen orientation convention; reversing orientation corresponds to inversion on the group variable.)
- Oriented plaquette set: \(P(\Lambda)\).

**Counting identities (hypercubic torus).**  
For periodic hypercubic \(\Lambda\) in dimension \(d\),
\[
|E(\Lambda)| = d\,|V(\Lambda)|,
\qquad
|P(\Lambda)| = \binom{d}{2}\,|V(\Lambda)|.
\]
Hence
\[
\frac{|E(\Lambda)|}{|P(\Lambda)|}=\frac{2}{d-1},
\qquad
\text{so in }d=4:\ \frac{|E|}{|P|}=\frac{2}{3}.
\]
Define the comparison constant
\[
C_{E/P}:=\frac{2}{d-1}
\quad (\text{so }C_{E/P}=2/3\text{ when }d=4).
\]

### A.2 Plaquette perimeter and bounded overlap constants
- Plaquette perimeter:
  \[
  m_\partial := |\partial p| = 4
  \quad\text{for any plaquette }p\in P(\Lambda).
  \]
- Bounded overlap constant \(\nu\): each oriented link \(\ell\in E(\Lambda)\) appears in the boundary word of at most \(\nu\) oriented plaquettes.
  On the \(d\)-dimensional hypercubic lattice,
  \[
  \nu \le 2(d-1),
  \qquad\text{so in }d=4:\ \nu \le 6.
  \]

### A.3 Link adjacency and link-graph distance
- Link adjacency \(b\sim b'\): \(b\sim b'\) iff there exists a plaquette whose boundary contains **both** links (possibly as inverses).
- \(\mathrm{dist}_E(b,b')\): graph distance on \(E(\Lambda)\) induced by \(\sim\).
- For link sets \(A,B\subseteq E(\Lambda)\),
  \[
  \mathrm{dist}_E(A,B):=\min\{\mathrm{dist}_E(b,b'):\ b\in A,\ b'\in B\}.
  \]


## B. Gauge group, representation, and geometric radii

### B.1 Group and Lie algebra
- \(G\): compact Lie group.
- \(\mathfrak g\): Lie algebra of \(G\).
- \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\mathfrak g}\): fixed \(\mathrm{Ad}\)-invariant inner product on \(\mathfrak g\).
- \(g_G\): associated bi-invariant Riemannian metric on \(G\).
- \(d_G\): Riemannian distance on \((G,g_G)\).
- \(\exp:\mathfrak g\to G\): Riemannian exponential at \(\mathbf 1\).
- \(\log\): local inverse of \(\exp\) on a ball about \(\mathbf 1\).
- \(\iota_G := \mathrm{inj}_G(\mathbf 1)\): injectivity radius at the identity.

### B.2 Fixed faithful unitary representation and the constant \(\lambda_\rho\)
- Fix a faithful unitary representation \(\rho:G\to U(n)\).
- Let \(d\rho:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak u(n)\) be the derived representation.
- Define the \(\mathrm{Ad}\)-invariant symmetric bilinear form
  \[
  B_\rho(X,Y):=-\Re\mathrm{Tr}\big(d\rho(X)\,d\rho(Y)\big),
  \qquad X,Y\in\mathfrak g.
  \]
  Faithfulness of \(\rho\) makes \(B_\rho\) positive definite on \(\mathfrak g\).

**Representation scale constant (conditional on simplicity).**  
When \(\mathfrak g\) is simple (and in particular for \(G=\mathrm{SU}(N)\)), all \(\mathrm{Ad}\)-invariant inner products are proportional, so there exists a scalar \(\lambda_\rho>0\) such that
\[
B_\rho(X,Y)=\frac{1}{\lambda_\rho}\,\langle X,Y\rangle_{\mathfrak g}.
\]
(If one chooses \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\mathfrak g}=B_\rho\), then \(\lambda_\rho=1\).  
For \(G=\mathrm{SU}(N)\) in the defining representation and the convention \(\langle X,Y\rangle_\lambda=\lambda(-\mathrm{Tr}(XY))\), one has \(\lambda_\rho=\lambda\).)

### B.3 Trace potential and its Taylor constants
- Define the single-plaquette trace potential
  \[
  \Phi_\beta(V):=\beta\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{n}\Re\mathrm{Tr}(V)\Bigr),
  \qquad V\in G,
  \]
  where \(\mathrm{Tr}(V)=\mathrm{Tr}(\rho(V))\).

**Local Taylor data at \(\mathbf 1\).**  
There exist constants \(r_{\mathrm{Tr}}>0\) and \(C_{\mathrm{Tr}}<\infty\) (depending only on \((G,\rho,g_G)\)) such that for all \(Y\in\mathfrak g\) with \(|Y|\le r_{\mathrm{Tr}}\),
\[
\Phi_\beta(\exp Y)=\frac{\beta}{2n\lambda_\rho}\,|Y|^2+\mathcal E_\beta(Y),
\qquad
|\mathcal E_\beta(Y)|\le C_{\mathrm{Tr}}\beta|Y|^3.
\]
In particular,
\[
D\Phi_\beta(\mathbf 1)=0,
\qquad
D^2\Phi_\beta(\mathbf 1)[Y,Y]=\frac{\beta}{n\lambda_\rho}|Y|^2.
\]


## C. Configuration manifold and cochains

### C.1 Configuration space
- Configuration manifold:
  \[
  M_\Lambda := G^{E(\Lambda)}.
  \]
- Vacuum configuration:
  \[
  U^{(0)}_b = \mathbf 1\quad\text{for all }b\in E(\Lambda).
  \]
- Product Riemannian metric on \(M_\Lambda\): \(g_\Lambda\) (factorwise equal to \(g_G\)).
- Riemannian distance on \(M_\Lambda\) induced by \(g_\Lambda\): \(d_\Lambda\).

### C.2 Cochain spaces and norms
- \(\mathcal C^k(\Lambda;\mathfrak g)\): \(\mathfrak g\)-valued \(k\)-cochains on \(\Lambda\) (with the chosen orientation convention).
- \(\ell^2\) inner product on \(\mathcal C^k\):
  \[
  \langle X,Y\rangle_{\mathcal C^k} := \sum_{c\in \text{\(k\)-cells}} \langle X_c,Y_c\rangle_{\mathfrak g}.
  \]
- Corresponding norm: \(|X|_{\mathcal C^k}^2 := \langle X,X\rangle_{\mathcal C^k}\).
- Sup norm (used for “small-field” control):
  \[
  |X|_\infty := \max_{c} |X_c|_{\mathfrak g}.
  \]

### C.3 Coboundary operators and Maxwell operator
- \(d_0:\mathcal C^0\to\mathcal C^1\): discrete coboundary (gradient).
- \(d_1:\mathcal C^1\to\mathcal C^2\): discrete coboundary (curl).
- Adjoints \(d_0^\*,d_1^\*\) are with respect to the \(\ell^2\) cochain inner products.
- Discrete Maxwell operator on 1-cochains:
  \[
  \mathsf M_1 := d_1^\*d_1 \quad\text{on }\mathcal C^1(\Lambda;\mathfrak g).
  \]

**Vacuum horizontal space.**  
Define
\[
H^{(0)}:=\ker(d_0^\*)\subseteq \mathcal C^1(\Lambda;\mathfrak g).
\]


## D. Wilson action and small-field region

### D.1 Plaquette holonomy
For \(p\in P(\Lambda)\), the plaquette holonomy map is
\[
U\mapsto U_p(U)\in G,
\]
given by the ordered product of the four link variables around \(\partial p\) (with inverses on oppositely oriented edges).

### D.2 Wilson action and Gibbs measure (finite volume)
- Wilson action:
  \[
  S_{\Lambda,\beta}(U):=\sum_{p\in P(\Lambda)} \Phi_\beta\big(U_p(U)\big),
  \qquad U\in M_\Lambda.
  \]
- Finite-volume Gibbs measure:
  \[
  \mu_{\Lambda,\beta}(dU) = Z_{\Lambda,\beta}^{-1}\,e^{-S_{\Lambda,\beta}(U)}\,dU,
  \]
  where \(dU\) is the product Haar probability measure on \(G^{E(\Lambda)}\).

### D.3 Canonical small-field set and smooth version
- Linkwise small-field set:
  \[
  K_\Lambda(r):=\prod_{b\in E(\Lambda)} B_r^G(\mathbf 1)
  =\{U\in M_\Lambda:\ d_G(U_b,\mathbf 1)<r\ \forall b\}.
  \]
- Geodesic ball around vacuum in the product manifold:
  \[
  \Omega_\Lambda(r):=B_r^\Lambda(U^{(0)})=\{U\in M_\Lambda:\ d_\Lambda(U,U^{(0)})<r\}.
  \]


## E. Small-field radii and Wilson Hessian stability constants

### E.1 Geometric radii from BCH/log control
Fix once and for all a radius \(r_\star\in(0,\iota_G)\) so that \(\log\) is well-defined on \(B_{r_\star}^G(\mathbf 1)\).

Let \(r_0>0\) be a radius small enough for the uniform BCH remainder estimates used in the plaquette-holonomy expansion.

Define the small-field cutoff
\[
r_{\mathrm{sf}} := \min\left(\frac{r_\star}{4},\ \frac{r_0}{4},\ \frac{r_{\mathrm{Tr}}}{4}\right).
\]

### E.2 Third-derivative control for the plaquette trace defect
Define the trace defect on four group elements:
\[
F(g_1,g_2,g_3,g_4):=\Re\mathrm{Tr}\big(I - g_1g_2g_3^{-1}g_4^{-1}\big),
\qquad (g_i\in G),
\]
with \(\mathrm{Tr}=\mathrm{Tr}\circ\rho\) by Convention 0.

Define the uniform third-derivative bound (operator norm):
\[
M_3(r_\star)
:=
\sup_{g\in B_{r_\star}^{G^4}(\mathbf 1)}
\big\|D^3F(g)\big\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]

### E.3 Wilson Hessian perturbation constant \(R_W(r)\)
For \(0<r\le r_{\mathrm{sf}}\), define
\[
R_W(r)
:=
\Big(\frac{\beta}{n}\Big)\,\big(2\nu\,M_3(r_\star)\big)\,r.
\]


## F. Haar/Ricci constant and the massive Maxwell operator

### F.1 Haar/Ricci lower bound constant \(c_H\)
Let \(c_H\ge 0\) denote a uniform Ricci lower bound for the product metric \(g_\Lambda\), i.e. a constant such that
\[
\mathrm{Ric}_{g_\Lambda}(U)\ \succeq\ c_H\,I
\quad\text{as quadratic forms on }T_UM_\Lambda,\ \text{for all }U\in M_\Lambda.
\]
**Conditional note (structure of \(c_H\)).**  
If the gauge group \(G\) has strictly positive Ricci curvature with respect to the chosen bi-invariant metric (e.g. no torus factor / semisimple case), then \(c_H>0\). If \(G\) has an abelian factor, \(c_H\) may be \(0\).

### F.2 Parameters and operator
Define
\[
m^2 := \frac{c_H}{2},
\qquad
\alpha := \frac{\beta}{n\lambda_\rho}.
\]
Define the “massive Maxwell” operator on 1-cochains:
\[
M := m^2 I + \alpha\,d_1^\*d_1
\quad\text{on }\mathcal C^1(\Lambda;\mathfrak g).
\]
On the vacuum horizontal space \(H^{(0)}=\ker(d_0^\*)\) define the restriction
\[
M_H := M\big|_{H^{(0)}}.
\]

### F.3 Kernel notation for \(M_H^{-1}\)
For oriented links \(b,b'\in E(\Lambda)\),
\[
(M_H^{-1}\eta)_b=\sum_{b'\in E(\Lambda)} (M_H^{-1})_{b,b'}\,\eta_{b'},
\qquad (M_H^{-1})_{b,b'}\in \mathrm{End}(\mathfrak g).
\]
Operator norm on kernel blocks:
\[
\|(M_H^{-1})_{b,b'}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
:=\sup_{|v|_{\mathfrak g}=1}|(M_H^{-1})_{b,b'}v|_{\mathfrak g}.
\]


## G. Combes–Thomas constants (finite-range inverse decay)

Let \(A\) be a self-adjoint finite-range operator on \(\ell^2(V;\mathsf H_0)\) for some graph \(V\) and finite-dimensional Hilbert space \(\mathsf H_0\).

Constants used in the Combes–Thomas inverse-decay lemma:
- Positivity gap \(a_0>0\): \(A\succeq a_0 I\).
- Interaction range \(R\in\mathbb N\): \(A_{xy}=0\) when \(\mathrm{dist}(x,y)>R\).
- Off-diagonal row-sum constant
  \[
  B:=\sup_{x\in V}\sum_{y\neq x}\|A_{xy}\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
  \]
- Combes–Thomas decay rate
  \[
  \eta:=\frac{1}{R}\log\!\Bigl(1+\frac{a_0}{2B}\Bigr).
  \]
