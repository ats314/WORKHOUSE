## What Is In This Folder

The Maxwell folder studies the **discrete Maxwell operator** - the Laplacian on 1-forms (gauge fields).

### Key Results

1. **Hodge Decomposition:** Discrete analog of ∇×∇× structure
2. **Calladine-Maxwell Rigidity:** Counting constraints from topology
3. **Massive Maxwell:** Adding mass term stabilizes infrared
