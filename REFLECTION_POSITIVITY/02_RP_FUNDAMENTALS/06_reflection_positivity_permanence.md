# Reflection positivity permanence under reflection-equivariant coarse graining and projective limits

## 1. Setup

Let $\Omega$ be a measurable configuration space equipped with an involution $\theta:\Omega\to\Omega$ (the OS reflection).  Let $\mathcal A$ be an algebra of bounded measurable observables, and let $\mathcal A_+$ be the positive-time subalgebra.

A probability measure $\mu$ on $(\Omega,\mathcal A)$ is **reflection positive** if
\[
\langle F, F\rangle_{\mu,\theta}:=\int \overline{F}\,\theta F\,d\mu\ge 0
\qquad\text{for all }F\in\mathcal A_+.
\]

## 2. Reflection-equivariant Markov kernels preserve reflection positivity

Let $K$ be a Markov kernel from $(\Omega,\mathcal A)$ to $(\Omega',\mathcal A')$:
\[
(Kf)(\omega):=\int f(\omega')\,K(\omega,d\omega'),
\]
and denote the pushforward measure $\mu':=K_*\mu$.

Assume:

1. **Reflection equivariance.** There is an involution $\theta'$ on $\Omega'$ such that
   \[
   K(\theta\omega, A)=K(\omega, (\theta')^{-1}A)\quad\text{for all measurable }A\subseteq\Omega'.
   \]
2. **Positive-time mapping.** $K$ maps positive-time observables to positive-time observables: $K(\mathcal A'_+)\subseteq\mathcal A_+$.

### Lemma 2.1 (permanence under coarse graining)
If $\mu$ is reflection positive on $\mathcal A_+$, then $\mu'=K_*\mu$ is reflection positive on $\mathcal A'_+$.

*Proof.* For any $F'\in\mathcal A'_+$, let $F:=KF'\in\mathcal A_+$.  Using equivariance,
\[
\int \overline{F'}\,\theta'F'\,d\mu'
=\int K(\overline{F'}\,\theta'F')\,d\mu
=\int \overline{KF'}\,\theta(KF')\,d\mu
\ge 0.
\]
\hfill $\square$

## 3. Permanence under projective limits on cylinder algebras

Let $(\Omega_n,\mathcal A_n,\theta_n)$ be a projective system of measurable spaces with compatible reflections and coarse-graining kernels $K_{n\to m}$ for $n\ge m$.  Let $\mu_n$ be reflection-positive measures on $\Omega_n$ such that $(K_{n\to m})_*\mu_n=\mu_m$.

Let $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{cyl}}$ be the cylinder algebra generated by pullbacks of observables from finite levels, and let $\mu$ be the projective limit measure on the cylinder $\sigma$-algebra.

### Lemma 3.1 (projective-limit permanence)
If each $\mu_n$ is reflection positive on $(\mathcal A_n)_+$ and the kernels are reflection-equivariant and positive-time preserving, then the projective limit $\mu$ is reflection positive on $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{cyl},+}$.

*Proof sketch.* Any cylinder observable $F$ depends on some finite level $n$, so reflection positivity reduces to that of $\mu_n$ by consistency.
\hfill $\square$

## 4. Why this matters for OS reconstruction

Reflection positivity is the input that produces a Hilbert space and a self-adjoint Hamiltonian via Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction.

The lemmas above isolate a structural mechanism: any continuum construction built as a projective limit of reflection-equivariant Markov coarse-grainings will preserve the OS inner product on the cylinder algebra.
